## 1. Purpose

This checkpoint persists the Product and architectural footing reached after adversarial review of the emerging Growth / Modified Wheel lifecycle specimen.

The immediate objective is not to complete the Wheel lifecycle, define a universal ontology, or design implementation. It is to preserve enough shared meaning to begin the next bounded design step without semantic drift:

> **For the first bounded covered-call lifecycle specimen, what exact evidence and derived facts are minimally required to distinguish every materially different tactical answer without guessing?**

The discussion reached a stopping point: further abstract refinement would add little unless a concrete operator question now pulls on the semantics.

---

## 2. Wheelwright boundary remains unchanged

Wheelwright is a governed financial domain server and evidence appliance. It may observe, store, search, compute, and evaluate deterministic rules.

It does **not** trade.

It does **not** exercise ungoverned fuzzy judgment.

Brokerage remains responsible for order construction details where appropriate and for execution. A human, agent, or another explicitly authorized decision layer remains responsible for judgment where Product has not defined a deterministic policy.

The useful boundary is:

> **Evidence and governance establish what Wheelwright may truthfully conclude. Wheelwright projects those conclusions into useful tactical language without strengthening them.**

---

## 3. Question-driven design remains the current North Star

The governing Product direction remains:

> **Start with a legitimate operator or agent question, attempt to answer it with governed Wheelwright capabilities, and work backward from missing facts, rules, or capabilities.**

The system becomes more capable as the operator's important unanswered-question list gets shorter.

The latest refinement is:

> **Questions determine the bounded claims worth answering. Evidence and governance determine which claims can be established. Operator projection communicates those claims without strengthening them.**

Question-driven design therefore discovers more than data requirements. It can also expose missing policy, missing authority, missing search completeness, or a claim that properly remains outside Wheelwright.

---

## 4. "Foolish" is discovery language, not a canonical predicate

The Principal's Growth Wheel discovery language is:

> **Keep my shares until it is foolish to keep them.**

The review established that "foolish" may arise from materially different causes and must not collapse them.

At minimum distinguish:

1. **Continued-holding eligibility** — may the existing inventory remain held under applicable governance?
2. **Acquisition eligibility** — may additional or replacement inventory be acquired?
3. **Intervention admissibility / economics** — which alternatives addressing the existing obligation satisfy policy, and at what cost or consequence?

These can differ.

A holding can remain permitted while later reacquisition is prohibited, for example because a concentration rule treats retention and new acquisition differently.

Likewise, the underlying can remain desirable while defending ownership through a particular short-call obligation becomes uneconomic or otherwise unacceptable.

Therefore:

> **"Defending this call is too expensive" may be one operator meaning of "foolish," but it does not imply that the underlying itself is no longer ownership-eligible.**

"Defense rationality" remains operator language unless Product later defines a deterministic comparative rule.

No new `FOOLISH` predicate is established by this checkpoint.

- Candidate / Alternative;
- Counterfactual Consequence;
- Action;

The existing model also already establishes a particularly relevant distinction:

> **A Wheel is an Operating Program spanning multiple positions or cycles; it is not one stable economic construction.**

The existing predicate-resolution work is also richer than the provisional PASS / FAIL / UNRESOLVED / NOT_APPLICABLE shorthand. Where applicable, preserve distinctions such as:

- `SATISFIED`;
- `NOT_SATISFIED`;
- `UNKNOWN`;
- `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT`;
- `AUTHORITY_MISSING`;
- `POLICY_UNDEFINED`;
- `NOT_EVALUATED`;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

These existing semantics do not automatically become v2 Product behavior.

The v2 clean-slate rule remains:

> **Do not inherit v1 behavior, mechanisms, or policy merely because an existing semantic noun exists.**

The complementary rule established here is:

> **Clean slate must not become semantic amnesia.**

A bounded v2 semantic counterpart should therefore emerge by **reconciliation and narrowing from surviving specimens**, not by designing a new ontology from scratch.

The immediate Product work does not require completing that semantic counterpart first.

## 6. Reuse versus defer

The following distinctions are strong enough to deliberately reuse as v2 vocabulary unless a specimen falsifies them:

- Account versus Account Regime;
- governed inventory / quantity versus symbol identity;
- Mandate / purpose versus Operating Program;
- Constraint versus Preference versus Outcome Stance;
- Observation versus authoritative governance assertion;
- Alternative versus possible consequence versus action / execution / confirmed outcome;
- effective economic time versus recorded knowledge;
- established negative versus insufficient evidence versus missing authority versus undefined policy versus dependency-blocked evaluation.

The following remain reconciliation input, not v2 commitments:

- exact Capital Pool / Capital Boundary / Allocation topology;
- provisional `Intent Assertion` semantics;
- whether Candidate and Alternative require separate durable identities;
- generalized lifecycle identity across holdings and obligations;
- universal consequence representation;
- strategy-specific v1 membership, recommendation, or execution behavior;
- any generic ontology, policy, dependency, workflow, or state-machine engine.

## 7. Dual model: economic lifecycle and epistemic evaluation

The discussion converged on two different structures.

### Economic lifecycle

Economic evolution is temporal.

Quantity-bearing inventory, rights, obligations, and cash can undergo Events and Transitions over time. Wheelwright may later reconcile those changes from authoritative evidence.

A lifecycle is therefore better understood as scoped state/event/transition history than as a simplistic finite enum such as `OWNED` / `CASH`.

This matters because real specimens can contain partial quantities, multiple obligations, partial assignment, simultaneous shares and cash, and multiple governed inventory blocks.

A conventional state machine can be useful locally where Product explicitly defines transitions, but no general state-machine framework is implied.

### Epistemic evaluation

What Wheelwright can establish is dependency-oriented.

Illustratively:

> **evidence + authoritative governance → derived facts → predicates / consequences → rule evaluations → governed assertions**

The arrows are not a universal execution order. Policy can define search scope; applicability can block derivation; consequences can feed rules; independent facts can support multiple claims.

The durable distinction is:

> **State/event/transition semantics describe economic evolution. Dependencies describe what Wheelwright can establish about that evolution from a specified knowledge set.**

Economic history and Wheelwright's knowledge of that history are not identical. An Event can occur before Wheelwright receives or reconciles authoritative evidence of it.

## 8. Same Wheel mechanics, different governance

The Growth Wheel and the existing Assignment-Centric Wheel provide a strong falsification pair:

> **Same or substantially similar mechanical topology, different governance.**

A Wheel can mechanically resemble:

> cash → CSP → assignment → shares → covered call → call-away → cash → repeat

without that topology defining the objective of the capital.

### Assignment-Centric / income-oriented specimen

The current use case associates this specimen with taxable income-producing capital.

Its governing purpose emphasizes production of distributable income. Assignment and turnover can be normal or desirable lifecycle outcomes.

This checkpoint does **not** establish that "Income Wheel" and "Assignment-Centric Wheel" are universal synonyms or that all income-oriented Wheels maximize premium or turnover.

### Growth specimen

The current use case associates the Growth specimen with Roth IRA capital.

Its governing purpose emphasizes long-term appreciation, with option premium as an overlay. Ownership is preferred while continuing to defend it remains acceptable under explicit policy.

A share surrender-price floor is a candidate policy parameter that can constrain newly assumed obligations. It is not a universal options primitive and its advancement semantics remain unresolved.

If defending ownership becomes unacceptable while the underlying remains acquisition-eligible, call-away can be tolerated and future re-entry interest can remain.

### Important non-implication

Do not infer:

> `ROTH => GROWTH_WHEEL`

or:

> `TAXABLE => ASSIGNMENT_CENTRIC_WHEEL`

Account type is context. It may help explain why a Principal selects a particular mandate or program, but it does not establish that governance automatically.

The specimen currently supports this decomposition as a useful question set:

- **Account** — where the assets reside;
- **Account Regime** — accounting/execution context of that account under existing terminology;
- **Portfolio Mandate / purpose** — what the governed capital is meant to accomplish;
- **Operating Program** — the recurring Wheel process;
- **Policy / Constraint / Preference / Outcome Stance** — how that governed scope behaves at decision boundaries.

Whether Growth and Assignment-Centric / income-oriented behavior are two Operating Programs or configurations within a broader Wheel family remains unresolved and need not be decided before the first bounded lifecycle specimen.

Continued interest in an underlying after call-away implies some governance may need to survive an individual holding or obligation. It does **not** establish that old inventory floors, membership, or eligibility automatically transfer to reacquired shares.

## 9. Tactical operator projection is a Product requirement

A specification question such as:

> Given identified inventory, an existing covered-call obligation, effective policy, and a declared evidence scope, which replacement alternatives are admissible, rejected, or unresolved?

is useful internally but is not operator-friendly.

The operator's natural question is closer to:

> **What are my moves with this call?**

Wheelwright should be able to project rigorous internal conclusions into concrete tactical alternatives, while preserving conditions and limitations.

Illustrative tactical language only — not public enums or ratified commands — includes concepts such as:

- roll / replace;
- wait to a defined review boundary;
- close;
- allow natural resolution.

The operator projection must not conceal:

- undefined policy;
- unresolved evidence;
- the horizon of a "wait" claim;
- uncertainty about assignment or other future outcomes;
- limitations of the searched alternative families;
- executable-market uncertainty;
- broker feasibility that has not been established.

A concise Product principle survives review:

> **Project governed conclusions into concrete tactical alternatives while preserving their conditions, consequences, scope, and limitations.**

This does not authorize Wheelwright to choose the "best" move unless Product has defined a deterministic rule that establishes a recommendation.

"What should I do?" can remain a natural operator entry question. Wheelwright must distinguish a policy-defined Recommendation from a set of established permissible alternatives that still require caller choice.

## 10. Governed qualitative projection

The exploratory phrase **ontological adjectives** produced a useful design discipline, but the broader term **governed qualitative projection** better captures the concern.

The proposal is not an adjective taxonomy.

Words such as `IDEAL`, `RIPE`, `URGENT`, `ATTRACTIVE`, `CHALLENGED`, or `ACTIONABLE` were examples only and are not ratified vocabulary.

The governing idea is:

> **When Wheelwright communicates consequential qualitative meaning, that meaning must not arise accidentally in UI prose, an LLM prompt, sorting, emphasis, an icon, or another presentation mechanism.**

Where a qualitative characterization is materially consequential, Product must determine whether it is:

- a governed semantic assertion;
- a sanctioned projection of a more precise assertion;
- capability-specific presentation vocabulary;
- caller judgment;
- or language that should be removed.

Determinism is necessary for a Wheelwright-derived classification but is not sufficient to justify its meaning. A reproducible formula can still encode arbitrary judgment.

A useful trigger for scrutiny is:

> **If changing the characterization could materially change what the operator understands, prioritizes, or does, its meaning probably cannot be left entirely to presentation discretion.**

Compression must retain access to the governing definition, material reasons, limitations, and evidence lineage. A qualitative projection must not imply a stronger comparison universe, predictive claim, or degree of optimality than Product has established.

This checkpoint establishes the **discipline**, not any particular qualitative classification.

## 11. "Complete enough" means bounded claim coverage

Completeness is not possession of every potentially useful datum.

The working definition is:

> **A capability is complete enough when it can distinguish the materially different findings in its declared scope, establish each finding from sufficient evidence and explicit semantics, and expose the specific reason any finding cannot be established.**

A second useful test is:

> **Could Wheelwright distinguish every materially different answer promised by the bounded operator question without guessing?**

Returning `UNRESOLVED` or equivalent for everything is truthful but does not prove a useful capability. Representative adequately evidenced cases must produce the distinctions the capability claims to support.

Completeness has at least three concerns:

### Evidence completeness

The required observations, authorities, identities, effective/recorded times, freshness or temporal suitability, and population coverage must be adequate for the bounded claim.

### Search completeness

The declared candidate universe, filters, exclusions, limits, pagination/unfinished work, and enumeration coverage must support any absence claim.

"None found" is weaker than:

> **No candidate satisfies policy within this completely evaluated declared scope.**

### Policy completeness

Applicable rules, parameters, composition semantics, alternative-family scope, and treatment of blocked or not-applicable predicates must be sufficient for the requested conclusion.

A statement such as:

> **No compliant move exists**

is stronger than:

> **No compliant replacement call exists**

because the former claims coverage over all move families included in the Product question.

"Without guessing" means, at minimum:

- no unstated defaults;
- no invented scope continuity;
- no implicit permission;
- no unsupported claim of search completeness;
- no strengthening estimates into facts;
- no hidden policy;
- no presentation language that exceeds the established assertion.

Explicitly labeled estimates and assumptions are permitted when the capability semantics allow them and their uncertainty is preserved where material.

## 12. The remaining scope decision is now made for the next specimen

The previous review identified one remaining blocker before evidence enumeration:

> Is the first question about replacement calls, or the broader tactical move set?

For Product discovery, the first bounded question will be:

> **For this identified inventory and existing covered-call obligation, what tactical alternatives can Wheelwright currently establish under the applicable governance and evidenced market state?**

Natural operator shorthand:

> **What are my moves with this call?**

This scope remains deliberately bounded:

- one identified governed inventory scope;
- one existing covered-call obligation;
- current applicable governance;
- current evidenced market/account state;
- no complete Growth Wheel lifecycle;
- no re-entry design;
- no portfolio optimization;
- no generic policy engine;
- no automatic brokerage execution.

Replacement-call analysis is one subordinate capability needed to answer the broader tactical question. It is not the Product question itself.

This avoids the false conclusion that "no compliant replacement" means "no compliant move."

---

## 13. Next artifact: bounded answer inventory before data inventory

The next design step is no longer another general architecture review.

Create a bounded **answer inventory** for:

> **What are my moves with this existing covered call?**

The answer inventory should enumerate the materially different claims the capability must be able to distinguish, including positive, negative, blocked, incomplete, and policy-dead-end findings.

Illustrative categories may include roll/replacement alternatives, close, time-bounded non-intervention, natural resolution, no compliant move in a sufficiently evidenced declared scope, and inability to establish a tactical answer because evidence, authority, applicability, search completeness, or policy is insufficient.

These examples are not yet a public enum or contract.

For every claim in that inventory, ask:

> **What exact evidence, authoritative governance, search coverage, and deterministic derived facts are minimally required to establish this claim without guessing?**

That evidence/derivation mapping is the next bounded Product/architecture exercise.

## 15. Milestone disposition

The Product/architecture discussion has reached sufficient shared semantic footing to move down one level of abstraction.

The durable working model is:

> **Questions determine bounded claims. Existing semantics are reconciled rather than blindly inherited or reinvented. Economic lifecycle and epistemic evaluation remain distinct. Governance can cause the same mechanical Wheel state to support different tactical conclusions. Wheelwright projects established conclusions into useful tactical language without strengthening them. Completeness is bounded claim coverage, not universal data accumulation.**

The next bounded question is:

> **For the first existing-covered-call tactical specimen, what findings must Wheelwright distinguish, and what exact evidence and derived facts are minimally required to establish each one without guessing?**

No implementation authority is created by this checkpoint.
