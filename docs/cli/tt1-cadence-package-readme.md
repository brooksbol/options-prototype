# TT1 daily cadence evidence

Upload this ZIP to ChatGPT and ask it to resume the TT1 19-session operator experiment, review held positions/exits, compare the observed put verticals and incremental portfolio exposure, and identify a candidate, alternative or WAIT for operator verification.

- market-and-verticals.json: normalized observed puts and every retained lower-strike pair for the experiment's 14 ETF research candidates. Short put signed delta −0.30 to −0.20;38–45 calendar DTE; no width cap or long delta filter. All eligible expirations are preserved, preferred closest to 45 identified. Excluded nonpositive/invalid credits remain visible.
- symbol-liquidity.csv: the actual v1 Options Market Liquidity observed calculation and shared browser CSV schema, applied to the full snapshot universe with the same 38–45/puts/0.20–0.30 filters. Default table sort, all rows, no symbol/measured-only narrowing. Per-width quoted/listed counts are evidence, not executable vertical quotes.
- tt1-account-positions-orders.json: separate observed broker reads, existing tt live derived projections, actual fills/orders, P50 and 21-calendar-DTE objectives, and retained/current balances. Opening fills, working orders and fills closing positions are different facts. A GTD expiration does not execute a time exit.
- evidence-coverage.json: read outcomes, per-source clocks and missing evidence.
- manifest.json: schema, session/export dates, source generation/revisions, filters, checksums of the other five files and completion status. The manifest does not checksum itself.

Observed facts retain their source fields/clocks. Pair credits, maximum loss and credit/risk are derived. Natural credit=short bid minus long ask. Midpoint credit=short midpoint minus long midpoint. Neither establishes complex-order execution. Candidate dollar values use a CONDITIONAL 100 multiplier: verify actual contract identity, multiplier and deliverable. Fees/assignment complications are excluded. Missing data stays null; normalized v1 zero OI/volume can conceal missing source observations (BUG-055); no invented replacement or grade. Provider Greek-time strings have no assigned timezone. Chain acquisition age is not individual quote age or independently timestamped spot. No new freshness threshold overrides backend admissibility.

Current BP is established only from a same-day timestamped direct broker balance response, never historical retained BP. Reads are non-atomic and BP may change. Verify actual available BP, broker per-position/order BP, contract terms, live complex markets, costs and P50 tick rounding in Tastytrade before deciding. Committed BP must not be subtracted again from available BP.

P50 closes at 50% of original maximum credit; time exit 21 calendar DTE, whichever occurs first. At most one new qualified short put vertical per trading session; WAIT is valid. No trade recommendation or broker instruction is issued by this command. All execution remains operator-controlled.

SPY/QQQ/IWM are not independent downside risks just because tickers differ. No correlation matrix, empirical return expectancy or portfolio risk score is supplied. Quantitative joint-risk analysis requires qualified aligned histories and a separately specified method. Historical XSP research is execution-model-sensitive and does not establish TT1 profitability.

Held-trade BP: `tt1-account-positions-orders.json` now includes `heldCapital`, the raw broker margin report under observed reads, and per-position `brokerHeldCapital` bindings. The broker GET is `/margin/accounts/{account_number}/requirements`. It reports gross margin separately from current group `buying-power` (with the original effect). The latter is reconciled against account used options BP and NLV minus available BP. `actualBrokerBuyingPowerRequirement`, when present, specifically means that current exact-matched group BP footprint; its basis is explicit. It is not the historical opening BP debit or guaranteed cash/BP released by a closing fill.

Bindings require exact signed contract quantities, established source clocks/currency, reconciled account data and no working-order inclusion. Netted groups, mixed structures, changing observations and ambiguous membership remain reported as broker risk groups without an invented allocation to individual trades. `includes-working-order=false` never means no closing orders exist. Original opening BP debits and prospective closing BP release stay unknown. The daily command never runs candidate simulations, including during missing-data fallback.

Modeled P50 probability is separate from an exit condition. Each derived held structure has `p50Probability: null` and `p50ProbabilityEvidence.status: "NOT_AVAILABLE"`: no exact authorized broker API source was established in the October29 investigation. Probability units, if a source is established later, are percentage points0–100; null means unavailable, never0%. Source and observation time remain null when no probability was observed. Historical TLT83%/IWM87% UI observations, POP, delta and the legacy tt `p50` boolean are not current modeled probabilities. The legacy boolean only indicates that a working target matches half the opening credit.

`p50ClosingDebit` is the existing opening-credit/2 calculation, per share, in the opening credit's price units; it equals `p50Objective.closingDebitPerShare`. It is a derived gross-profit target, not a probability, order price, fill, or fee-adjusted result. Existing order evidence remains separate. An entry-ticket P50 for today's hypothetical price would not establish remaining probability of hitting a held position's original-credit target. No broker P50 formula/model inputs or held-position refresh semantics were found in the published API. Independent estimation requires a separately specified and validated model; none is supplied. P50 probability is also not an established success probability for TT1's combined P50-or-21-DTE rule.

October30 market freshness correction: during the broker-declared regular equity session, the exporter reads all14 experiment ETF nested catalogs and batches every eligible38–45DTE standard put through Tastytrade's existing REST quote endpoint (at most99 options plus their underlying per call). This is a bounded standalone read adapter: no v1 force/observe request, v2 acquisition, grant or scheduler change. On429/401/403 it stops, without retry or credential escalation; caps/failures and missing coverage are explicit. Contract catalog identity, standard100-share deliverable profile and same-expiry geometry are checked; no delta filter is applied to protective puts and no width menu is used.

`market-and-verticals.json.sourceMode` distinguishes `BROKER_CANDIDATE_SNAPSHOTS`, `OFF_HOURS_RETAINED_TEST_ONLY` and `NO_CURRENT_BROKER_MARKET_EVIDENCE`. Broker candidate rows carry quote `updated-at`, per-batch underlying bid/ask-midpoint spot and its update time, request receipt, raw quote/spot evidence and qualification issues. The regular-session operational check reuses v1's existing provisional two-minute quote-fresh window: **both legs and spot** must have nonfuture explicit-zone row timestamps within that window and within the current broker session. This is a row-age bound, not a universal trading-safe threshold or an independent Greek-clock guarantee. Greeks are taken unchanged from the same broker response row; a separate Greek calculation timestamp/model is not supplied. Cross-provider/model equivalence, atomic observation and executable spread pricing are not established. Tastytrade final verification remains necessary.

Retained v1 chains are always preserved (`retainedMarketEvidence` when broker snapshots are primary), with original quote/Greek values and per-chain/source clocks. They remain useful after hours, weekends and failed/denied reads, but are `RETAINED_TEST_ONLY` with `RETAINED_NOT_CURRENT_TRADE_EVIDENCE`, never current qualified opportunities. Newly downloading an old chain does not refresh its Greeks. Missing source clocks remain missing; Tradier's zone-unspecified `greeksUpdatedAt` is preserved verbatim and never assigned UTC. `symbol-liquidity.csv` still reconciles exactly with the accepted v1 table at the retained generation; it does **not** describe the separately refreshed broker snapshot universe and cannot confer current eligibility. Its unchanged liquidity grades must not override market-row exclusions. Available account BP, held BP and all existing position/exit calculations remain separate and unchanged.
