NAME
    ww history — inspect brokerage transaction evidence

SYNOPSIS
    ww history [--account ACCOUNT] [--table | --tsv | --csv | --jsonl]
               [-v | --verbose] [--no-color]
    ww history --help | -h | --man

DESCRIPTION
    Show trades and money movements through Account-scoped backend history.
    Without --account, discover authorized Accounts and compose their nested
    history reads. With --account, select a nickname such as tt1 or pts;
    operators never need GUIDs. Backend grants enforce each Account's access.
    One failed Account does not suppress successful independent results.

    tastytrade: backend policy may acquire and persist history when needed;
    reuse eligible retained evidence otherwise. No separate ww fetch is
    needed. Source paging, OAuth, evidence admission and persistence belong
    to the backend. Read-through must be enabled there; the CLI never contacts
    a brokerage. Unavailable/stale results and failed refreshes remain explicit.

    Fidelity: read previously admitted activity CSV evidence. No hidden
    local CSV import or brokerage fetch. The last import time is not broker
    freshness. Exact row-equivalence identity does not establish unique broker
    events, corrections or reversals. Missing currency/amounts remain unknown.
    Use existing ww sync account ACCOUNT to submit available supported CSVs.

OUTPUT
    Terminal: compact table, signed cash values, separate commissions and
    reported fees, preserved order references for multi-leg trade records.
    No strategy grouping, realized P/L, tax or performance accounting.
    Unknown is ?, never zero. Cash credits/debits are not profit/loss.
    Aliases cyan, Fidelity green, tastytrade red, symbols yellow, prices orange;
    neutral cash signs, white details and alternating dark row backgrounds.

    Default pipe: headerless TSV, one transaction per record; tabs/newlines
    and control characters are escaped. --csv: RFC-style quoting, header,
    exact decimal strings and provenance; aggregate output has one header.
    --jsonl: one transaction per line with per-Account history context.
    --table forces human output; --verbose includes complete human details
    unless an explicit machine format is selected. --no-color, NO_COLOR or
    TERM=dumb disables human color. Machine output is uncolored.

    Context/diagnostics go to stderr. Successful empty history emits no
    TSV/JSONL records; CSV retains its header. Independent acquisition clocks,
    observed date span, source lineage and status survive machine formats.
    A complete bounded result never establishes complete lifetime history.
    Source snapshot consistency remains unverified even after full paging.

LIMITS
    Backend full scans: at most 100 pages / 25,000 source records / 16 MiB,
    with a shared bounded provider deadline. Complete admission is atomic;
    failed scans preserve prior admitted evidence and retain safe source pages.
    Oversized responses fail explicitly; there is no silent truncation or
    public continuation cursor in this slice. No local transaction ledger.

EXIT STATUS
    0  Complete available bounded results, or empty authorized Account scope.
    1  Incomplete result or operational failure; successful Account records
       may still be present. Read stderr and each record's history context.
    2  Usage/configuration error.

EXAMPLES
    ww history
    ww history --account tt1
    ww history --account pts --verbose
    ww history --csv > history.csv
    ww history --account tt1 --jsonl > tt1-history.jsonl
    ww history --account tt1 --table | less -SR
