### Settled cash source continuation

The [native balances reference](https://developer.tastytrade.com/reference/balances-and-positions/getAccountsAccountNumberBalances/) distinguishes `cash-balance` (settled cash, possibly negative) from buying power and pending cash. The adapter consumes only explicitly reported USD cash; it does not value a sweep fund at an assumed NAV or calculate margin. Source availability has not been checked against a live Account.

- `TastytradeClient.settledCashBalance` uses the existing bounded, secret-screened read-only transport for the requested Account. `PositionAcquisition` retains the exact response and processing attempt in the existing Artifact/import owners (`API_BALANCES`), separately from securities. No second holdings store or synchronization resource is introduced.
- `TastytradeCashBalance` requires reported Account/currency, decimal precision and source-envelope validity. Source `updated-at`, source day and backend receipt are distinct. An unavailable/invalid source clock remains absent; receipt does not become source time.
- `PortfolioCashCustody` admits independently readable signed custody evidence. Positive cash has a Cash Construction and principal basis; zero/negative balances do not become positive owned exposure. Missing cash cannot be replaced by derivative buying power or pending cash. Negative cash is inspectable without a margin implementation.
- `PortfolioReconciliation` retains known security balances when a companion cash source fails, but qualifies overall completeness. Residual inventory never establishes buying power. Snapshot discovery includes the independently timed cash source and observation outcome.
- Single directly sourced inventory constituents can satisfy their own membership predicate; this does not admit a multi-leg package. Restricted projections must downgrade validated recognition when its association authority is unavailable. Original immutable Constructions remain unchanged.

`PortfolioCashCustodyTest` uses synthetic source envelopes through real loopback HTTP, SQLite retention, normalization, reconciliation and controller projections. Its five checks cover cash principal and provenance, missing/wrong-Account/currency/HTTP failure, zero/negative amounts, missing/future source clocks, and independent custody/Position grants. It does not establish live source behavior or installed grants. Public source upload cannot manufacture the backend-only API_BALANCES provenance.
