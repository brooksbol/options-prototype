# Semantic obligations and falsification boundaries

Candidate rules; no production evaluator is implemented. JSON Schema checks structure; `verify.py` supplies an independent exact-arithmetic/reference-policy oracle over synthetic specimens. A later implementation must enforce these obligations on actual admitted evidence, not merely reuse the tests' labels.

1. **Occurrence versus evidence.** A unique Position UUID is independent of source series. CLOSED is terminal; a new opening uses a new UUID. Resource rules do not prove observed closure, causal fills or continuous ownership. Unknown continuity stays qualified; split/recombine and governed-scope identity remain open.
2. **Immutable interpretation.** Construction and Model version bytes/meaning are write-once. All Model references identify an exact version. Predecessors form an acyclic same-Account/same-Position chain. Distinguish corrected knowledge from actual exposure change. Inline nested groups bind absolute quantities and may carry exact Model versions; they are not automatically independent Positions.
3. **Typed inventory.** Quantities are positive magnitudes plus side, never two conflicting sign conventions. Source inventory has Account/instrument/custody/evidence-cut identity, not lifecycle authority. No repeated owned allocation; collateral/protection references do not allocate inventory again. OTHER leaves preserve uninterpreted supported source units and cannot acquire standard payoff claims.
4. **Global conservation.** At each admitted Account evidence cut, sum every current owned allocation by source inventory, side, unit and currency. Include residual once; exclude historical predecessors from current sums. Reject/flag over-allocation or conflicting cuts. Missing allocation coverage is unknown, not balanced. Residual is not proven available. Basis/fees/history/collateral preserve attribution and source relationships under recomposition; sums alone prove no lot policy.
5. **Recognition.** A validated name requires evidenced membership, correct role kind/side/ratio and all applicable nested underlying, expiry, currency, multiplier, deliverable, strike-order and coverage constraints. A reverse Jade Lizard's short call must be above the put spread. Failure retains composition with null validated model, explicit reason and candidate hypotheses. Missing name does not prevent mathematically valid generic evaluation.
6. **Payoff.** Standard option intrinsic uses signed actual quantity * multiplier * max(S-K,0) or max(K-S,0). Shares are linear; cash constant. Gross P/L adds Account-signed attributed opening cash flow to signed terminal liquidation value, before fees. Cash that merely secures a put is a relationship, not another invented option cashflow. Adjusted deliverables require an explicit supported payoff definition; this candidate refuses exact adjusted valuation rather than pretending all deliverables are 100 shares. Mixed expirations need horizon-specific unexpired valuation; no unique intrinsic polyline is asserted.
7. **Exact coordinates.** Backend emits a continuous piecewise affine function on nonnegative underlying price, exact decimal/rational coefficients, explicit common-expiration date, currency, aggregate quantity basis, boundaries and breakevens. Intervals are [lower,upper), null upper is +infinity. Shared boundary values agree. Broken-Wing example tails are -700 and -1700 USD with +300 apex; glyph height loss never changes these values. This is terminal economics, not early exercise simulation or an executable quote.
8. **Current economics.** Broker-reported marks/P&L preserve their field origin. A reported open-P/L field with unverified fee/basis convention uses brokerReportedOpenPl, not an unqualified grossUnrealizedPl. Derived close values require validated entire-package evidence and explicit midpoint or whole-package mark methodology. Total package money is distinct from per-unit order premium. Missing opening fills leave credit/P&L/original DTE unknown; a working limit is not a fill. Stale or mixed-source assumptions remain qualified, never silently become exact current value.
9. **Orders.** Requested actions, reported filled quantity and actual reported fill records are distinct. Order creation/working/cancel/expiry alone changes no held exposure. Match targets using evidence, not symbol coincidence. Complete supported order coverage is needed to assert no protective stop. Trigger and execution limit are separate; provisional stop interpretation promises neither activation nor protection. GTD manual checkpoint is not a timed exit or verified notification tactic.
10. **Clocks and absence.** Source observation, day-precision export, receipt and evaluation target are different. Today's quote cannot substitute for missing historical evidence. A returned valid resource may have incomplete/unsupported/conflicting analytics. Operational success, import status and economic completeness remain separate.
11. **Warnings and access.** Every displayed result retains status, method, assumptions, evidence and applicable warnings across summary/expanded projection. URI-only includes no financial conclusion. Source/audit/historical/constituent links require applicable target grants; User ownership and guessed IDs confer none. Original multi-account bytes require all represented Account rights. The offline policy oracle is not runtime security certification.

They are not an API service, generic production recognizer, source-admission implementation, live brokerage integration, complete continuity engine or Product acceptance. `tt-live-replay.test.mjs` exercises actual transitional source without network; imported legacy tests use their own controlled source stubs. ## Corrected monetary convention (supersedes ambiguous earlier opening-cost prose)

All financial amounts declare currency and units. Cash flows use Account perspective: positive received, negative paid. Opening credit is positive openingCashFlow; opening debit negative. Closing sale produces positive closing cash flow; buying back a short produces negative. estimatedClosingCashFlow is a current liquidation estimate, not an execution. Quantities are positive magnitudes plus LONG/SHORT side, multiplied exactly once by the applicable verified multiplier. Source price magnitudes have explicit PER_UNDERLYING_UNIT/PER_CONTRACT/TOTAL_LEG or PACKAGE_TOTAL sizing; UNKNOWN prevents money derivation.

Allocation.basis is a nonnegative magnitude of attributable share acquisition cost or option premium excluding fees. LONG cash flow=-basis; SHORT cash flow=+basis. Securing cash is retained principal and contributes no invented premium or profit. Gross unrealized P/L for the stated remaining open composition equals attributed openingCashFlow + estimatedClosingCashFlow. Positive fee magnitudes are subtracted for net results; unknown fees keep net unknown. Realized P/L requires actually documented closed/expired/disposed exposure with supported opening attribution; a working Order cannot establish it. Broker-reported P/L is preserved separately and never relabeled gross/net without a verified dialect.

Original/current/cumulative comparison is optional. Original share investment basis is distinct from broker tax basis and from economic break-even. Documented interval premiums/closing cash flows/fees do not establish a lifetime total if history is partial. Covered-call example: premiums2250, actual closing cashflows-800, fees22 produce1428 documented net option cash flow; ten closed calls realize1200 gross/1180 net. Current call remains unrealized (+250 opening,-100 estimate,+150 gross). Economic BE85.72 USD/share uses the explicitly documented interval and original10000 USD investment; synthetic reported tax basis remains10000. Current all-package P/L additionally includes supported share value change.

Protective-put floors use the stated share investment basis and the premium of that particular protection period. Original95 strike/300 premium implies -800 historical gross floor; replacement90 strike/400 premium implies -1400 for the stated current package, excluding previously paid premiums and fees. The old floor is HISTORICAL_ONLY. Lifetime all-in floor is not silently asserted. LEAPS call paid2000/sold2500 yields500 realized gross/498 net with2 fees; replacement premium3000 is separately paid. Neither call establishes shares owned before exercise.

The historical examples supply explicit synthetic continuity and retained original/current Construction snapshots. Intermediate roll holdings are not reconstructed from cash-flow events. Detailed histories may be absent or qualified without invalidating valid current custody. `runtime-invariants.md` is the required cross-resource/source-truth register. No tax-lot allocator, assignment selection, operator association or Strategy engine is implied.
