# Principal mandate — Product-first continuous engineering

Date: October 13, 2026
Status: Principal-stated mandate; operational workflow changes require reconciliation with existing authority.

Working software must drive architectural learning and reach the Principal early, not last. AI engineering agents should accelerate throughput while preserving Wheelwright's architectural qualities and established Product expectations. The Principal defines the outcome; agents own routine design, implementation, tests, corrections and integration. Escalate actual missing capabilities, genuine Product ambiguity, and explicit authority boundaries—not ordinary engineering difficulty.

A review rejection invalidates the candidate, not the Product goal. The desired operating model is a continuous engineering and independent-falsification loop within explicitly delegated scope, with early operator-visible software and a distinct authorization boundary for consequential operational changes. Repeated related falsifiers trigger model/invariant review, not endless narrow patches. Measure time to first working specimen, time to Principal-accepted operational software, and Principal interruptions.

This record does not itself grant implementation retries, deployments, evidence acquisition/admission, grant changes, or other consequential mutation authority. Existing governing controls remain in force until reconciled and amended through their authoritative mechanisms.

Provenance: Principal direction in the October 13, 2026 PL-CONV-01 conversation; see docs/00-project-charter.md, docs/foundations/three-actor-model.md, docs/foundations/death-spiral-avoidance-protocol.md, and docs/WARNING-operating-model-reality-report-FINAL.md.

## Continuous proof and architectural conservation — Principal clarification, October 13, 2026

This extends, rather than replaces, Product-first continuous engineering. Proof starts during Product discovery and accompanies Architecture, Design, Implementation and Delivery; it is not deferred until independent release review. At every consequential assertion, recover relevant ratified guardrails and working Product precedents; prefer RECOVER → REUSE → COMPOSE → EXTEND → INVENT. New abstractions carry a specific, falsifiable capability-gap justification. Existing architecture is a constraint, not merely reference material.

The work item carries a small durable index of Product specimens, applicable invariants, reuse decisions, gaps, falsifiers/fitness functions, and independently checkable evidence. The index is not itself proof. Product discussions test observable operator outcomes and reuse; architecture tests authority/ownership and composability; design tests contracts and counterexamples; implementation runs structural and behavioral fitness functions; delivery verifies candidate identity and installed behavior. A later stage inherits earlier established claims without gratuitously reopening them; contradictory new evidence revisits only affected claims.

Guardrail retrieval and applicable proof checks must be invoked by the engineering substrate, not depend on an agent remembering to do so or claiming compliance. Mechanically verifiable boundaries require externally controlled checks; judgment-heavy claims require evidence-backed independent falsification. An implementer cannot waive its own obligations, silently weaken fitness tests, or certify an exception. An inability to find existing capability does not prove its absence.

This is a mandate to establish enforceable proof-carrying work, not a claim that current tools already enforce it. Reuse the existing technology-quality fitness program and staged WW-GATE-001 research; do not build a parallel bureaucracy. A staged gate is not active enforcement. Empirically test the substrate with conforming and deliberately nonconforming candidates, fresh-agent recovery, test-bypass attempts and measured elapsed time. If checks are skippable or expensive enough to delay early working Product observation, the mechanism fails its purpose.

Engineering proceeds continuously within actual delegated authority. Proof checks should be proportional and incremental, not serial Principal approval gates. A reviewer REJECT invalidates a frozen candidate and informs model synthesis; retry still requires whatever authority the current enforcement mechanism actually grants. Consequential evidence and operational changes remain separately authorized. Changing those rules requires explicit authoritative reconciliation, not reinterpretation of this mandate.

**Immediate regression specimen:** `ww show positions` must discover the accepted `tt live` visual precedent, existing Position/association semantics (Doc 58, Doc 70 and current v2 contracts), and broker-owned execution boundaries before proposing new renderer or order-lifecycle machinery. Verify that failure to recover those precedents is detected before substantial implementation. This is a proof-of-enforcement requirement, not another document acknowledgement.


## Principal clarification — smallest thin slice (October 22, 2026)

**Principal exact wording:** “the simplest thin slice is a hello world.”

**Scope:** Wheelwright-wide engineering methodology, **not limited to PL-MCP-01, MCP, a particular client, or a particular workstream**. This reinforces the Product-first mandate's demand for the earliest useful working observation; it is not a new standalone ratified Principle or permission to bypass established authority.

**Operational interpretation (actor inference, not additional Principal quotation):** Begin with the *simplest genuinely executable end-to-end “hello world”* that establishes basic wiring, boundaries, feedback, and observable behavior, before adding domain complexity. A hello world can prove connectivity or execution flow; **it does not prove** business-semantic correctness, authorization, privacy isolation, fitness, acceptance, or readiness for deployment. Increase scope only when the next question or falsifier requires it. Where even a hello world would cause protected changes or disclosure, authorization and safety constraints still apply.

**Reconciliation and recoverability:** Applies under the existing Product-first, reuse-first, early-observation and proof-carrying engineering guidance. Future actor bootstraps must recover this through the engineering mandate, rather than treating the phrase as a special-case PL-MCP-01 specification. Associated PL-MCP-01 discovery is merely one application. No new execution or implementation authorization is created by recording this clarification.


## Principal clarification — trading operations continue while Product evolves (October 29, 2026)

**Principal exact statements (October 29, 2026):**

> "the real tension is between day to day tactical needs and building a durable product"
>
> "i make trades every day. i will do that with hacked together command line tools but i'd rather do that with a fully functional product. evolving from one to the other is the ongoing activity that should go on above the day to day tactical needs. i have a monthly income obligation. that doesn't go away just because i want architectural goodness"
>
> "it's ok if the work we're doing is reusable."

**Product operating direction:** Daily trading is the Principal's continuing operational activity, not an engineering milestone or a dependency on unfinished Talk functionality. Product engineering continuously evolves those existing working workflows into an integrated, durable Wheelwright Product. The Principal's income obligation does not pause for architecture or engineering. This is a constraint on engineering prioritization and delivery, **not** an instruction to force trades, override risk/WAIT decisions, or relax authorization, evidence quality or financial semantics.

**Reconciliation with existing Product-first mandate (engineering interpretation, not a new Principal quotation):**

- Protect currently working tactical/broker/CLI procedures during Product development. Do not require the Principal to wait for parity before continuing ordinary trading; do not alter or retire a working tool until its replacement has demonstrable operator acceptance.
- Let actual trading operations supply Product specimens, capability gaps and priorities. Reconcile them through existing canonical intake/defect/authority mechanisms rather than creating a parallel backlog.
- Prefer vertical increments that improve a real operator workflow **and** leave behind reusable governed domain capabilities. When an increment is infrastructure-only or presentation-only, make that distinction explicit; do not manufacture a second financial engine to claim reuse.
- Evaluate delivery on two separate axes: (1) immediate tactical operator value, and (2) durable financial meaning reusable by other clients. Do not call API/tool success alone Product acceptance, and do not require full historical accounting proof for independent tactical facts where governing contracts admit them.
- Financial certainty remains explicit: distinguish broker-reported facts, qualified operational estimates and established continuity/accounting results; new estimate semantics require appropriate Product decisions.
- Protect the standing daily operating workflow and monthly income context without turning financial pressure into mandatory deployment, greater risk, or unauthorized broker execution. WAIT remains a legitimate outcome.

**Codex/engineering handoff:** Recover this clarification at every relevant Wheelwright Product/architecture bootstrap. Interpret the ongoing work as progressive migration of proven daily trading workflows into reusable Wheelwright capabilities—**operate every day; improve continuously; replace only when accepted as better**. Use accepted `tt live`, v1 Deployment, brokerage execution evidence and real Talk sessions as precedents. Continue ordinary authorized engineering without repeated Principal interruptions; reserve consequential grants, deployment, credentials, acquisition-policy changes, trading and unresolved Product semantic choices for their existing authority boundaries.

**Standing and limits:** This preserves an explicit Principal Product direction within the existing mandate. It does not itself authorize a new implementation workstream, deployment, grant change, acquisition, brokerage trade, or acceptance of financial inference. Reconcile against current higher-precedence Product authority if a conflict arises.


## Principal UX clarification — no agent CYA language in the operator UI (October 31, 2026)

**Exact Principal wording:**

> "my UI is not a playground for an agent's CYA language. CYA language is visual and cognitive noise and should be avoided."

**Context:** Principal observation of the Wheelwright Talk covered-call tactical view. Financial inputs and reviewed calculations had been recovered, but the operator-visible card was crowded with repetitive methodological disclaimers, provenance narration, source age/completeness paragraphs, and other actor self-protection language. The Principal values the financial result and its truthful qualification; the objection concerns unnecessary verbal and visual overhead in the tactical foreground.

**Product-facing direction (interpretation; not additional Principal quotation):** The operator UI is a tactical decision surface, not an agent compliance report. Show position identity, meaningful state, financial outcome, thresholds and graphics first. Do not spend the operator's attention budget on repeated CYA boilerplate, lengthy self-justifications, full ISO timestamps, generic caveats, or verbose narrations of how the result was produced. Move supporting accounting assumptions, source lineage, warnings and explanations into concise, discoverable, normally collapsed details/evidence, while keeping the *minimum material qualification needed to avoid a misleading primary claim* visible. Avoid redundant qualifications; use purposeful source/age and estimate labels rather than paragraphs. Unsupported facts remain explicitly unknown where their absence affects the operational answer. Real authorization denials, materially unsafe ambiguity, consequential uncertainty and other necessary warnings must still be communicated clearly; hiding them merely to produce a clean UI is not authorized.

**Reconciliation:** This reinforces, rather than replaces, the existing Product-first low-cognitive-load mandate, the PL-MCP-01 financial-answer UX goal (W3), the accepted 'answer first; evidence on demand' treatment, and the Principal's prior 'no disclaimer noise; hide any of that under accordion links' specimen recorded in BUG-052. ADR-024 and the Evidence Appliance's financial/provenance authority, epistemic precision and disclosure rules remain unchanged. No new financial or trading semantics are ratified by this UX clarification.

**Recoverable acceptance question:** Can the operator interpret the tactical situation at a glance without reading agent CYA prose, while finding material evidentiary limits and full provenance on deliberate inspection? If the answer requires decoding repeated disclaimers, the interface has failed; if hiding a limit turns an estimate into an apparent certain/current fact, it has also failed.

This is a documented Principal Product/UX requirement, not permission for unrelated code changes, deployment, grants, acquisition or brokerage actions.
