Date: October 13, 2026
Status: Principal-stated mandate; operational workflow changes require reconciliation with existing authority.

Working software must drive architectural learning and reach the Principal early, not last. AI engineering agents should accelerate throughput while preserving Wheelwright's architectural qualities and established Product expectations. The Principal defines the outcome; agents own routine design, implementation, tests, corrections and integration. Escalate actual missing capabilities, genuine Product ambiguity, and explicit authority boundaries—not ordinary engineering difficulty.

A review rejection invalidates the candidate, not the Product goal. The desired operating model is a continuous engineering and independent-falsification loop within explicitly delegated scope, with early operator-visible software and a distinct authorization boundary for consequential operational changes. Repeated related falsifiers trigger model/invariant review, not endless narrow patches. Measure time to first working specimen, time to Principal-accepted operational software, and Principal interruptions.

This record does not itself grant implementation retries, deployments, evidence acquisition/admission, grant changes, or other consequential mutation authority. Existing governing controls remain in force until reconciled and amended through their authoritative mechanisms.

## Continuous proof and architectural conservation — Principal clarification, October 13, 2026

This extends, rather than replaces, Product-first continuous engineering. Proof starts during Product discovery and accompanies Architecture, Design, Implementation and Delivery; it is not deferred until independent release review. At every consequential assertion, recover relevant ratified guardrails and working Product precedents; prefer RECOVER → REUSE → COMPOSE → EXTEND → INVENT. New abstractions carry a specific, falsifiable capability-gap justification. Existing architecture is a constraint, not merely reference material.

The work item carries a small durable index of Product specimens, applicable invariants, reuse decisions, gaps, falsifiers/fitness functions, and independently checkable evidence. The index is not itself proof. Product discussions test observable operator outcomes and reuse; architecture tests authority/ownership and composability; design tests contracts and counterexamples; implementation runs structural and behavioral fitness functions; delivery verifies candidate identity and installed behavior. A later stage inherits earlier established claims without gratuitously reopening them; contradictory new evidence revisits only affected claims.

Guardrail retrieval and applicable proof checks must be invoked by the engineering substrate, not depend on an agent remembering to do so or claiming compliance. Mechanically verifiable boundaries require externally controlled checks; judgment-heavy claims require evidence-backed independent falsification. An implementer cannot waive its own obligations, silently weaken fitness tests, or certify an exception. An inability to find existing capability does not prove its absence.

This is a mandate to establish enforceable proof-carrying work, not a claim that current tools already enforce it. Reuse the existing technology-quality fitness program and staged WW-GATE-001 research; do not build a parallel bureaucracy. A staged gate is not active enforcement. Empirically test the substrate with conforming and deliberately nonconforming candidates, fresh-agent recovery, test-bypass attempts and measured elapsed time. If checks are skippable or expensive enough to delay early working Product observation, the mechanism fails its purpose.

Engineering proceeds continuously within actual delegated authority. Proof checks should be proportional and incremental, not serial Principal approval gates. A reviewer REJECT invalidates a frozen candidate and informs model synthesis; retry still requires whatever authority the current enforcement mechanism actually grants. Consequential evidence and operational changes remain separately authorized. Changing those rules requires explicit authoritative reconciliation, not reinterpretation of this mandate.

Verify that failure to recover those precedents is detected before substantial implementation. This is a proof-of-enforcement requirement, not another document acknowledgement.
