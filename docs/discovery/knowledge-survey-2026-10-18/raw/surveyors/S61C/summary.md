7. Durable capital purpose exists even when no decision is pending (earmarked cash, reserves). Capital stays governed while idle (3145–3173). Idle capital is not failed capital (4402).
8. Inactivity semantics: WAIT/HOLD/LET RESOLVE/NO TRADE may not be synonyms. Insufficient evidence is not automatically WAIT (4400). This bears on the current posture vocabulary.
9. GDXJ covered-call lesson: AI supplied an unstated objective. The Principal's actual objective (recover basis via premium, then have the shares called away) changed the governed interpretation (4322–4324).
