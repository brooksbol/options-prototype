# Track A ETF option-ecosystem observation schema v0 — proposed

**Status:** Category E research contract proposed for Principal review, 2026-10-01. This is a field and aggregation specification, **not** authorization to acquire data, a completed Pass 0 census, a quality threshold, a score, a ranking, a candidate label, or a specimen selector. Canonical intake: `PL-RESEARCH-01`.

**Authority and population:** Consume frozen [Pass −1 v0](pass-minus-one-v0-occ-tradier-identity-snapshot-2026-10-01.md) without changing its 6,072-underlying and 6,381-class denominators. The [Track A product view](pass-minus-one-etf-cohort-and-tradier-ecosystem-view-2026-10-01.md) declares a **Cboe-labeled ETF projection** of 1,643 underlyings / 1,766 OCC ordinary class rows. A future observation limited to that projection must carry both its own and its parent denominators; it is **not** the complete 6,381-class [Pass 0 v1](pass-zero-v1-class-geometry-method-2026-10-01.md) census. The 445 OCC-name ETF candidates and 290 still unclassified products retain their separate frozen evidence tiers. No current Tradier quote or chain data have been acquired for this projection by this document.

## Grain, keys and raw-value rule

The economic observation grain is **exact OCC option class × expiration × strike × observation date**. Persist `right` and exact provider `option_symbol` as additional identity keys: a put and call can share a strike, and a class can have multiple contract identities. `run_id` distinguishes multiple captures on one date. `observation_market_date` is the `America/New_York` local date of response receipt; the scheduled date is separate. One run must remain within one observation market date or be split into separately reported runs; never mix dates in one class rollup. UTC request/response and provider timestamps remain independent evidence and are never rewritten to imply simultaneity. A chain response queried by *underlying* does not make every root in its expiration union an observed OCC class.

All provider bodies are immutable raw evidence identified by SHA-256 and a storage path. A decoded provider field has `{presence, raw_json_value, parse_state, parsed_value}`. `presence` is exactly `ABSENT`, `NULL`, or `VALUE`; **numeric zero is `VALUE` with value `0`**, never null or missing. `parse_state` is `NOT_APPLICABLE`, `PARSED`, or `TYPE_ERROR`. Preserve source spelling and raw Greek update strings in the body even when a parsed UTC/number is also stored. A derived null always carries `null_reason`; a missing datum is not a numeric zero.

| Logical record and required key | Raw fields / provenance to retain | Derived fields allowed |

`source_json_pointer` plus raw-body hash is the lossless provenance for an individual contract row; decoded column values are a query convenience. The record model may be implemented as files or tables so long as keys, raw values, distinct states and lineage survive. No unmentioned default converts an absent root, field or request into zero contracts.

**Acquisition-protocol review correction:** The initial table could not represent a listed expiration when its chain request failed, and a run split into slots could omit an entire assigned class without a denominator row. Add the following narrow records and foreign keys before any capture. They preserve the same observation grain and do not change Pass 0 state semantics:

The [separate acquisition protocol](track-a-etf-ecosystem-acquisition-protocol-v0-2026-10-01.md) defines how these records are populated and when a partition or wave is complete.

| Required record/key | Contract |

## States and derived contract measures

`identity_observation_state` is one of `NOT_TESTED_THIS_RUN`, `PRIOR_IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED`, `EXACT_ROOT_OBSERVED`, `EXACT_ROOT_ABSENT_IN_COMPLETE_RESPONSE`, or `AMBIGUOUS_PROVIDER_ROOT`; an explicit alias edge is recorded orthogonally and the frozen Pass −1 state is never rewritten. `geometry_state` is one of `IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED`, `ACQUISITION_INCOMPLETE`, `EXACT_ROOT_NOT_OBSERVED`, `EXACT_ROOT_NO_FUTURE_PUTS`, `FUTURE_PUTS_NO_SAME_EXPIRATION_PAIR`, or `SAME_EXPIRATION_PUT_PAIR_OBSERVED`. Only complete, parseable, exact-root evidence may support the last three states. A valid empty response is distinguishable from transport, HTTP and parse failure. `quote_reference_state` and `history_reference_state` are **separate**, each `NOT_REQUESTED`, `ACQUISITION_INCOMPLETE`, `RAW_OBSERVED`, or `PARSE_UNUSABLE`; `terms_state` is independently `UNVERIFIED`, `VERIFIED_STANDARD`, or `VERIFIED_NONSTANDARD`. None of reference, terms or market quote quality changes `geometry_state`. The date/time convention for “future” versus same-date expiration must be declared before acquisition; it may not be chosen after observing a class's listings.

Class-state precedence is mechanical: unresolved exact-root identity → `IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED`; otherwise any required expiration/chain request not complete and parseable → `ACQUISITION_INCOMPLETE`; otherwise no exact root in the complete class-specific evidence → `EXACT_ROOT_NOT_OBSERVED`; otherwise evaluate future put presence and same-expiration pairs in that order. If one expiration shows a pair while another required request fails, retain the observed pair rows but report the class census as `ACQUISITION_INCOMPLETE`, not a completed pair census. The *required* expiration set and completeness test belong in the frozen acquisition protocol, not an after-the-fact interpretation. Pass −1 identity and ancillary reference states remain orthogonal.

The following derived values are descriptive and retain source IDs, formula version and a `null_reason` when not computable:

- **Geometry:** expiration date, `dte_days` in calendar days, unique put/call strikes per exact root and expiration, sorted adjacent strike gaps, minimum/maximum strike, and count of same-expiration put pairs with `K_short > K_long`. Separate past, same-date and future-dated expirations under the declared time convention. Use exact strike values; do not choose a target DTE, delta, width or pair.
- **Reference:** optional `strike_over_reference_price = strike / S` only when a separately declared positive timestamped `S` is interpretable; preserve unscaled strikes. No realized-volatility transform is implied by this schema.
- **Quote surface:** `bid_minus_ask` is **not** defined; `quoted_width = ask − bid` when both fields parse as numbers, including zero. `quoted_width_over_mid = (ask − bid)/((ask + bid)/2)` only when the midpoint is positive. Record the raw relation (`MISSING_OR_NULL_SIDE`, `TYPE_ERROR`, `NEGATIVE_SIDE`, `CROSSED`, `LOCKED`, `UNCROSSED_WITH_ZERO`, `UNCROSSED_POSITIVE`) without calling any one state tradeable. Compute each side's age from `received_at_utc − provider_side_timestamp` and the absolute bid/ask timestamp skew only when both times parse; preserve negative ages as clock/evidence anomalies. No age, spread or size threshold is adopted.
- **Pair surface:** for **all** same-class, same-expiration put contract pairs satisfying `K_short > K_long`, derive exact width and the states of the two close-debit sides (`short.ask`, `long.bid`) as well as full two-sided presence. Count pairs by exact width and strike identity, never by a selected best pair. The pair relation may be computed from retained contract rows without storing every Cartesian pair. A synthetic `short.ask − long.bid` may be reported when both sides are numeric, with raw leg IDs and timestamp skew; it is a component-market bound, **not** a package quote, fill or executable debit. Preserve zeros/nulls and never rescue a pair from another class/root/expiration.

## Permitted aggregation and denominator contract

1. **Class × expiration × run:** count raw and unique contracts by right; unique strikes, adjacent gaps and exact-width pair counts; quote-field presence and value-state counts; distributions of numeric width, side age, size, OI and volume with their non-null `n`; joint pair-side state counts with *all geometric pairs* as denominator. No one expiration or strike represents the class.
2. **Class × run:** retain the complete set of its expiration rollups and an explicit class state. Summaries may count expirations, puts/calls, pair-bearing expirations and their observed DTE range; they must show the number of successfully acquired exact-root expirations against those attempted. Do not turn “no 40–50 DTE expiry today” into a class failure.
3. **Underlying × run:** retain **every** frozen OCC class row and its state, plus per-class rollups. Count class/root multiplicity and unresolved/acquisition/reference/terms states. Do not pool contracts from ordinary and adjusted classes or average their quote statistics into one ETF grade.
4. **Partition × run; cohort × wave:** each run reports its assigned underlying/class denominator; the wave ledger reports the full 1,643/1,766 ETF denominators and 6,072/6,381 parent denominators. Count observations by exact state and source tier. Publish attempted and unattempted counts rather than silently shrinking either denominator. The 445 OCC-name candidates are a separately labeled projection, not members added to the 1,643 by implication.
5. **Across runs:** compare like exact roots/classes with dated provenance and record entry, disappearance, changed expirations/strikes, quote-state and pair-surface changes. A missing later request is **unknown**, not ecosystem decline. A changed expiration phase is shown, not normalized away. No persistence verdict follows from one date.

For each permitted numeric distribution in v0, emit `n`, `min`, `max`, and `p25/p50/p75` over finite parsed numeric observations; quantiles use the ascending nearest-rank value at index `ceil(p × n) − 1` for `p=0.25/0.50/0.75`, and all numeric summaries are null with reason `NO_NUMERIC_OBSERVATIONS` when `n=0`. Also emit raw-record count and separate `ABSENT`, `NULL`, `TYPE_ERROR`, zero, negative and positive counts. Exact strike/width values remain available; v0 defines **no DTE, moneyness, width or quality bins**, top-N summaries, or thresholded coverage rate. A later binning view would need its own predeclared version and could not discard out-of-bin rows. Rollups cannot rank ETFs, discard thin classes, label an ETF “actionable,” select a spread, or convert synthetic prices into Exit Reliability. Later stage-specific analyses may derive those conclusions only under separately accepted rules and evidence.

## Boundary to resolve before acquisition

Before any 1,643-ETF observation is authorized, freeze the capture schedule and slot semantics, complete request plan and retry budget, raw storage/hash layout, parser version, reference-history request specification and adjustment provenance, the same-date expiration convention, and how class-specific expiration completeness will be established. If terms or timestamps cannot be interpreted, retain raw evidence and the explicit state. This schema intentionally contains **no quality cutoff, ranking, specimen target, or crawl authorization**.
