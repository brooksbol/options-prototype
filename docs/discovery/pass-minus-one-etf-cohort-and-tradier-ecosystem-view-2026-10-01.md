The 1,643 are the directly reproducible answer to “which are ETFs?” **under the declared Cboe classification rule**. The additional 445 show why that list is not a claim of complete ETF coverage. For example, OCC calls `BEDZ` and `VICE` ETFs, but neither has an exact Cboe reference in the frozen files; their 2026-10-01 exact Tradier lookups were empty and their identities remain unresolved. The tier labels, Cboe company/settlement references where present, and OCC names remain attached to every row in the population export. Cboe's settlement label is reference metadata, not proof of an adjusted class's actual deliverable.

## What the archived Tradier evidence actually shows

The Pass −1 Tradier endpoint returned grouped option **symbol strings** for each queried underlying. Decoding an exact root's strings gives its listed expiration dates, put/call side, strike lattice, and root variants at the capture. These are provider-observed *listing topology*, not full chain observations or executable quotes.

| Dated lookup finding | Cboe ETF tier | OCC-name candidate tier |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Underlyings whose exact query returned option roots | 1,643 / 1,643 | 443 / 445; 2 empty/unresolved |
| OCC classes whose **exact class root** appeared | 1,741 / 1,766 | 451 / 453 |
| OCC class rows with root unresolved in lookup | 25 | 2 |
| Exact-root classes with at least one expiration containing two put strikes **in lookup strings** | 1,741 | 450; `SMCF` has one put strike at each of two expirations |
| Exact-root put / call contract strings | 283,936 / 283,940 | 36,074 / 36,074 |

Within the Cboe ETF tier, **113 underlyings have multiple frozen OCC classes** and **91 lookups exposed multiple Tradier roots**. Root count is an identity/structure observation, not an ETF-quality grade. `TSLL` and `TSLL1` illustrate why an ordinary root and an adjusted-looking root stay separate; the bounded chain pilot found that `contract_size: 100` did not encode `TSLL1`'s cash deliverable component. Of the 25 root-absent OCC classes, 24 have numbered suffixes and one has another underlying/class spelling difference; these are evidence cohorts, not automatic adjustment or exclusion decisions. All 1,741 exact-root Cboe ETF classes had a same-expiration put pair somewhere in their **lookup strings**, but that does not say a particular DTE window, same-day chain, risk location, quote, or execution opportunity exists. The 25 root-absent rows remain members, not failed ETFs.

For each ETF ecosystem, the available dated lookup can describe **class/root multiplicity**, expiration calendar and DTE distribution, put/call listing breadth, strike counts and spacing per exact root/expiration, and whether any same-root put pair is listed. This is more informative than a binary “optionable” flag and needs no preferred strike, delta, spread width, or market-quality threshold.

The adopted Pass 0 method would add a **fresh, dated, class-specific chain geometry observation** for every frozen class row, with separate identity/acquisition states and ancillary timestamped underlying quote/history. Raw Tradier chains can additionally preserve option identity, bid/ask and sizes, side timestamps, volume/OI and verbatim Greek update evidence for *later* market-quality research; those fields do **not** determine Pass 0 geometry state or select a specimen. The existing bounded pilots show this surface exists for a small contrast panel, not for this full ETF cohort. The Pass −1 lookup has none of the synchronized quote/history, chain freshness, authoritative adjusted deliverables, package-fill, or longitudinal evidence needed to infer liquidity or Exit Reliability.

## Chain-wide measurement contract before candidate discovery

The research question is **which option ecosystems repeatedly offer observable, comparable opportunity**, not which named ETFs should be favored now. Preserve raw evidence at `(OCC underlying, OCC class, exact Tradier root, observation time, expiration, right, strike)` grain. Report every roll-up with its underlying, class, expiration and acquisition denominators; a class-root miss, missing chain, stale side, and valid zero remain different states. The following measurements are descriptive and do not select a unique spread:

| Layer | Measure across the ecosystem | Meaning and stage boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Identity and contract terms** | OCC-to-Tradier root edges; number of classes/roots per product; ordinary versus potentially adjusted class; source-dated deliverable, multiplier, exercise/settlement and product-structure evidence. | An ordinary underlying match does not prove the desired option class. A suffix or Tradier `contract_size` does not prove adjusted payoff terms. Keep unresolved rows in every denominator. |
| **Listing geometry (Pass 0)** | Exact-root future expiration calendar and observed DTE; per-expiration put/call strikes, adjacent-strike gaps, strike range, same-expiration pair existence and range of representable widths; weekly/monthly labels where evidenced. Record geometry by expiration and also its distribution across dates, rather than pass/fail at 40–50 DTE. | Shows the *choice set* and its phase sensitivity before a trade is chosen. A union of roots or one expiration is not a class-specific ecosystem. Raw symbol lookup is only an earlier listing proxy; fresh chains establish the dated census. |
| **Ancillary reference** | Timestamped underlying quote, declared price history, corporate-action adjustment provenance, quote-to-chain time separation, and positive/usable reference-price state. Option strikes may be described as raw distances from spot, while all unscaled strikes remain preserved. | Enables later replay of independently declared risk coordinates. Reference gaps do not change Pass 0 geometry state; no preferred RV window or specimen follows. |
| **Raw market quality (later pass)** | For **every observed contract**, bid and ask values including explicit zero versus null; side-specific timestamps and age; displayed bid/ask sizes; last trade/time, OI and volume with their update semantics; Greek values and verbatim update strings when supplied. Summarize two-sided/usable quote coverage and distributions of absolute/relative width, age/skew, size and participation by exact class, expiration/DTE and strike-distance region. Also measure **joint** quote availability for same-root put pairs across predeclared width/location reporting cells, without choosing the best pair; synthetic leg-price bounds remain observations, not package fills. | These are observations of the phenomenon the funnel will later study, not Pass 0 gates or inputs to choose the comparable specimen. Preserve both raw rows and missingness; do not average all contracts together so a larger strike lattice dominates the product comparison. Tradier's [chain endpoint](https://docs.tradier.com/reference/brokerage-api-markets-get-options-chains) and [quote field reference](https://docs.tradier.com/docs/quotes) expose relevant fields, but their presence, freshness and comparability must be established in each capture. |
| **Stability and exit evidence (later passes)** | Repeat exact-class geometry and quote-quality observations on declared dates and intraday phases; track class entry/exit, expiration roll, strike-grid changes, quote coverage and quality drift. After a specimen and management rule are separately frozen, observe complete-position package fill/no-fill, time and concession when closure is required. | A single listing or quote snapshot cannot establish persistence, broker executability or Exit Reliability. Synthetic leg prices are not package fills. |

Keep product structure (broad-market, sector, bond, commodity/crypto trust, leveraged/inverse, single-stock exposure, etc.) as source-backed **characterization**, not an option-quality conclusion from the Cboe `ETF` tag or a ticker-name heuristic. For descriptive comparisons, predeclare observation times; if a later version introduces DTE/strike-distance reporting cells, declare them before seeing values and preserve raw rows outside them. Show the distribution and missingness within each class before aggregating to an ETF. Do not select the most liquid expiry or strike to represent the ETF: that would preselect the later outcome. A future Pass 1 selector may then choose a complete position from the recorded geometry and independent reference evidence without reading spread, size, OI, volume or package quality.

The [proposed Track A observation schema](track-a-etf-option-ecosystem-observation-schema-v0-2026-10-01.md) makes record keys, raw/derived fields, missing states, provenance and permitted class/expiration/underlying rollups explicit. It is a reviewable research contract, not acquisition authority.

**Research boundary:** Keep product-type evidence, OCC underlying identity, OCC option-class identity, and Tradier root observations as separate fields. Repeat a declared census on later dates to study ecosystem change; do not overwrite this dated view or treat absent exact roots, thin listing geometry, or missing Cboe metadata as permanent exclusions. No fresh Tradier acquisition or trade selection is authorized by this analysis.
