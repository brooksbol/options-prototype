# Wheelwright Talk — Conversational freedom and capability discovery

**Recorded:** 2026-10-23. **Standing:** Principal Product-direction refinement / discovery evidence, not new ratified domain semantics, execution authority, or authorization to implement.

## Principal direction and observed problem

Principal: “yes, i want more freedom with the kinds of questions i can ask. i want it to be more conversational. capability gaps should emerge from there.”

Principal also insisted: “yes but...i still want deterministic behavior.”

Concrete observed browser question: “show me my problem children” was rejected with guidance to ask a supported Account-trades inventory question. The user wants the chatbot to understand this kind of natural tactical question, compose authorized existing capabilities, offer meaningful partial findings, and surface genuinely missing capabilities rather than require pre-enumerated phrasings.

A TT1 preview initially withheld the entire response after a model explanation failed verification. A subsequent preview visibly kept the deterministic authorized trade table and disclosed that the generated explanation was withheld. **Evidence availability and explanation success must be distinct**; this is observed preview behavior, not proof of integrated-main capabilities.

## Product intent (interpretation, not ratification)

- Free-form operator conversation should shape capability discovery: operator phrasing should not be restricted to a hardcoded menu of accepted questions.
- Flexible intent interpretation and bounded authorized tool composition are separate from deterministic financial facts, calculations, assessments and access control.
- Equivalent operator intents applied to the same evidence and governing rules should produce equivalent substantive findings, even when phrasing and presentation differ.
- A model may interpret intent, propose a bounded evidence-gathering plan, and explain validated results; it must not invent holdings, inferred official Constructions, valuation, priority, configured management rules or certainty.
- Partial answers are preferable to blanket refusal when supported evidence suffices for a useful narrower observation. Show what was established, what was derived, what is inferred, and the **specific** missing evidence/capability needed for a stronger conclusion.
- A missing capability is an observation of unmet Product need arising from conversation; it is not automatically a new requirement, a mandatory MCP tool, or authorization to implement. Reconcile through existing subject owners and backlog/roadmap mechanisms.
- A probabilistic narrative failure should not suppress validated, authorized deterministic evidence. Independently indicate an explanation-generation or integrity-check failure.
- UI may use graphics, compact tables, typography, color and narrative to explain tactical findings; TT Live is the information-density benchmark. Complexity belongs behind “simple questions, hard answers that seem simple when you read them.”

## Examples / scope distinction

- “Show me my problem children” is an *operator-intent specimen* for account/portfolio-wide attention triage. Definition of governed attention classification, prioritization, scope and permissions remains to be established through Product work; this phrase alone is not an accepted financial-rule contract.
- “Why is EWZ one of them?” should retain the referent, explain evidence supporting any actual finding, and acknowledge unavailable evidence.
- “What would change your assessment?” can reveal a need for current execution-quality quotes, comparable history, working orders, Greeks or management-rule evidence.
- Recognized legs in TT1 do not by themselves prove broker grouped trades, realized economics or complete iron-condor/vertical Constructions.
