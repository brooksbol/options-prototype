# Pass 1 position-risk comparability: bounded synthesis and holdout handoff

**Status:** Exploratory research note (Category E), 2026-10-01. No selector, history window, risk tolerance, eligibility rule, or full Pass 0 acquisition is adopted.

**Canonical intake:** `PL-RESEARCH-01`. The accepted [Pass −1 snapshot](pass-minus-one-v0-occ-tradier-identity-snapshot-2026-10-01.md) and [Pass 0 v1 method](pass-zero-v1-class-geometry-method-2026-10-01.md) govern their respective stages. This note interprets the [bounded coordinate/history comparison](pass-one-coordinate-history-comparison-2026-10-01.md); it does not change either decision.

## Research target

Pass 1 needs a **complete put-credit-spread position** at an independently specified downside risk location, tenor, and protection geometry. A short put alone does not define the position: moving the long strike changes the loss region, gross contractual exposure, and net sensitivities. Risk comparability may need a declared *vector or bounded region*, not one scalar. It cannot imply equal assignment, settlement, gap, volatility, or execution risk across classes. Later Exit Reliability work asks whether the *whole position* can be exited at a defensible observed or executed price; entry geometry is only its sampling frame.

The recorded eight-underlying comparison does not select a rule. At 50 DTE all tested coordinates matched the same eight of nine ordinary exact-root rows. RV20 narrowed the matched short-leg delta span to 0.0672 from price-relative 0.1792, while net-position spans were 0.0535 and 0.0507 respectively. Seven of eight matched pairs changed across RV20/40/60. TSLL was unmatched under every rule; at 43 DTE exact-root SPX lacked puts while SPXW exposed them. Choosing RV20 from these diagnostics would tune to this panel. Neither a hybrid nor a preferred history window has an independent justification yet.

## Candidate position-level invariants

“Independent” below means the contract **selection** can ignore bid/ask spread, displayed size, OI, volume, and broker quality labels. Observing such fields later is still necessary for market-quality and Exit Reliability research. Pass 0's adopted geometry and ancillary quote/history can replay price/RV coordinates if the reference protocol is declared; it does not certify contract terms or supply a ready-made Greek model.

| Candidate coordinate | Risk property sought; quality independence | Evidence and Pass 0 replay | Uncontrolled risk, circularity, and adjusted-class limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Net position delta** (`long-put Δ − short-put Δ`) | Approximate *local* directional sensitivity of the complete spread. Greek values need not use liquidity fields, but provider Greeks may depend on option marks and volatility inferred from the same quote surface later judged for quality. | Exact two contracts, signed quantities, synchronized Greek values and provenance. Pass 0 may retain raw Greeks but does not require their presence or freshness for its state; the adopted reference bundle alone is insufficient. | Same net delta can conceal different leg locations, width, gamma, loss region, and payoff cap. Selecting on provider delta then testing delta dispersion is circular. Adjusted deliverables require authoritative multiplier/payoff mapping before aggregation. |
| **Joint short/long delta vector** | Holds approximate local location of *both* legs, rather than allowing offsetting sensitivities to produce one net value. It is still option-price-model dependent even if spread quality fields are ignored. | Per-leg Greek update times, conventions, exact class/root, quantities and deliverables. Not guaranteed by Pass 0 evidence contract. | Does not equalize nonlinear payoff, tail loss, width, tenor, or executable close. Using either leg delta as a selection input makes the same delta an in-sample diagnostic. Adjusted contracts have the same aggregation problem. |
| **Two strike distances from reference price**, e.g. `ln(Kshort/S)` and `ln(Klong/S)` | Holds downside onset and full-width location as fractions of today's underlying price. Independent of option quotes. | Exact-root strikes/expiration plus positive timestamped `S`; replayable from Pass 0 if quote field, age, and alias are declared. | Price-relative location ignores heterogeneous realized volatility, skew, jump risk and tenor. Width is encoded by the difference, but equal percentages need not imply equal loss probabilities. Raw strike-to-price mapping is unsafe for adjusted deliverables or class-specific units. |
| **Two RV-standardized distances**, e.g. `ln(K/S)/(RV × sqrt(T))` for each leg | Holds leg locations in independently measured historical-volatility units, at declared tenor. Independent of option-market quality if RV uses only underlying returns. | Pass 0 geometry, quote, enough dated history, adjustment convention, return formula/window, calendar basis and raw provenance. Replay is conditional on those being declared before capture. | Historical RV may miss forward volatility, jumps, skew and regime change; window choice materially changes contracts on the pilot. Comparing selector output with provider delta remains an *external* diagnostic only. Adjusted-class units and historical corporate actions require separate terms/provenance. |
| **Width normalized by price or RV scale** (`(Kshort−Klong)/S` or `/[S×RV×sqrt(T)]`) | Holds protection interval or gross strike exposure on a common price or historical-move scale. Independent of option quote quality. | Same strikes, `S`, tenor and optionally history as above; conditionally replayable. | Width alone does not locate the spread relative to spot, so it cannot define a specimen by itself. Normalization does not establish equal dollar loss, tail probability or executable risk. Adjusted deliverables may make a strike difference an invalid payoff proxy. |
| **Gross payoff cap / maximum-loss geometry** | For a verified standard unit vertical, width × authorized multiplier bounds the **gross** terminal spread payoff; a normalized cap can hold contractual loss scale. It is independent of market quality only **before** credit. | Exact legs plus authoritative deliverable, multiplier, settlement and exercise terms. Pass 0 records uncertainty and `contract_size` but does not verify terms. | Net maximum loss is `gross cap − actual credit`; broker capital-at-risk/margin and actual credit require market/execution/account evidence. Using quoted credit or broker BPR to choose the specimen would import later market quality. No standard payoff claim for adjusted classes without authoritative terms. |
| **Standardized downside region or scenario-payoff vector** | Holds both loss onset and degree of protection under predeclared price shocks or independently estimated return quantiles; could compare position *payoff shape* instead of a single Greek. A strike/underlying-history construction is quality independent. | Geometry, synchronized `S`, declared shock grid or sufficient adjusted return history; verified deliverables for dollar payoff. Pass 0 is conditionally adequate for standard-class strike regions, but not contract-term or probability-model certification. | Historical quantiles or parametric probabilities are model-dependent and unstable; risk-neutral probabilities would additionally need option prices and create selection/diagnostic circularity. A shock grid omits intraday path, exercise/assignment, mark-to-market Greeks and gaps beyond its endpoints. Adjusted classes require their actual payoff terms. |

An analytically specified *position region* could combine leg locations and width with a gross-payoff bound; this is a research hypothesis, not a third adopted rule. The older [credit-spread discovery](../39-credit-spreads-deployment-behavioral-discipline-discovery.md) motivates considering distance, width, delta, and maximum loss together, while the [options-domain contract](../foundations/options-domain-competence-contract.md) prevents treating a Greek as probability or `contract_size` as complete deliverable authority.

## One preregistrable holdout experiment for a future session

1. **Freeze the protocol before acquisition.** Publish its hash, selection code, numeric coordinate targets/tolerances, field semantics, two observation dates, history transformation, and falsification cutoffs. Do not derive any of them from the eight-underlying deltas or holdout diagnostics. Candidate definitions are limited to **(A)** the two price-relative leg distances and **(B)** the two RV-standardized leg distances, each with an explicitly bounded width and gross standard-contract payoff scale. This tests two independently interpretable coordinate families, not a sweep of tuned hybrids. Any numeric targets require independent economic rationale in the preregistration; the prior pilot's targets are not automatically inherited.
2. **Declare the holdout from frozen Pass −1 identities only.** Exclude the prior eight underlying products from diagnostic sampling, while keeping their rows in the frozen population. Within frozen source-metadata strata for ETF, ordinary equity, index, and metadata-unknown products, order eligible OCC `(underlying, class)` rows by SHA-256 of a published fixed salt plus their stable identifiers; take the first three per stratum. Add the first two numbered/adjusted classes and first two identity-unresolved classes by the same order as separate evidence-state controls. If a stratum has fewer than three rows, retain its shortfall; do not backfill using current chains, Greeks, or liquidity. Preserve all selected rows, including those that cannot produce a specimen. No provider alias may replace the exact class root silently. This is a small, declared class-row sample, not a new universe.
3. **Hold expiration identity fixed across phase.** Predeclare one standard monthly expiration and two future observation dates approximately 50 and 43 calendar days before it, with the same exact OCC/Tradier class root on both dates. Record actual DTE and any class-specific weekly/monthly labeling. No fallback expiration or nearby root is substituted; absence is an unmatched/evidence state. The two dates are a paired phase check, not a causal estimate of calendar effects.
4. **Declare reference and matching rules.** Capture a timestamped underlying quote and enough dated, adjustment-described history to compute **20- and 60-return** sample standard deviations of daily log returns, annualized by `sqrt(252)`; apply both windows as sensitivity factors, not as a post hoc winner search. Declare quote price field, age ceiling, chain/quote separation, bar adjustment and missing-bar handling before requests. For each exact class and expiration, choose the nearest strike to each leg target in that candidate's coordinate (lower strike wins a tie); require both within **one quarter of the declared leg-target separation** and `Kshort > Klong`, else report unmatched. No delta, spread-quality, or alternate-root rescue. The quarter tolerance is an experimental convention to be fixed before observation, not Pass 1 policy.
5. **Report the full result vector.** Show denominator and exact-root/identity/deliverable states first; matched and unmatched by stratum, date, coordinate and history window; selected contract IDs and changes across dates/windows; both leg locations, normalized width, and standard-contract gross payoff cap. On matched pairs only, report signed **net** delta plus each leg delta, gamma/vega/theta where timestamp and convention permit, and payoff under a preregistered downside shock grid. Report missing/stale Greeks separately. Greeks are diagnostics; bid/ask, size, OI, volume and other market-quality fields do not choose contracts. Adjusted classes remain separate unless authoritative deliverables permit payoff calculation.
6. **Predeclare interpretation limits.** A candidate's claim to *universal* specimen coverage fails if any selected exact-root ordinary class has no unique tolerance-compliant pair; report the cause, not an exclusion. A claim of robust identity across history windows fails when its selected contract pair changes for a selected class; do not hide the rate behind matched-only delta spans. A claim of cross-stratum comparability cannot be made for a stratum with no matched, contract-term-verified position. Even complete matches cannot prove equal exit quality, true probability of loss, or future stability. The holdout may falsify a coordinate's stated coverage/robustness claims or expose missing evidence; it cannot establish a winner by minimizing one dispersion statistic on this small sample. Further adoption would require independently declared practical tolerances and replication.

## Sufficiency and handoff boundary

The adopted Pass 0 ancillary quote/history contract is **sufficient in kind** to replay price-relative and historical-RV coordinates for exact-root ordinary classes, provided the future protocol declares a long enough history horizon, price adjustment, timestamp/freshness, source/version, bar gaps, and exact reference-price field **before** capture. It is not yet a guarantee that those fields have been captured or interpreted. An independently computed scenario return distribution needs the same history provenance; a risk-neutral probability or broker-capital selector would need additional evidence and would change the research question.

For **payoff or capital-at-risk claims**, the genuinely missing independent evidence is authoritative class-level contract terms: deliverable composition and cash components, multiplier, settlement/exercise convention, effective date and source version, joined to exact OCC/Tradier contract identity. Tradier `contract_size: 100` alone did not resolve `TSLL1` in the prior pilot. Keep term-unknown classes in the denominator with a separate state. Do not change Pass 0 geometry state, launch its full crawl, or choose a Pass 1 rule to fill this gap.
