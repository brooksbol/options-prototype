# Pass 1 position-risk comparability: bounded synthesis and holdout handoff

**Status:** Exploratory research note (Category E), 2026-10-01. No selector, history window, risk tolerance, eligibility rule, or full Pass 0 acquisition is adopted.

**Canonical intake:** `PL-RESEARCH-01`. The accepted [Pass −1 snapshot](pass-minus-one-v0-occ-tradier-identity-snapshot-2026-10-01.md) and [Pass 0 v1 method](pass-zero-v1-class-geometry-method-2026-10-01.md) govern their respective stages. This note interprets the [bounded coordinate/history comparison](pass-one-coordinate-history-comparison-2026-10-01.md); it does not change either decision.

## First-principles target: complete-spread downside liability shape

The defensible **research target for a future holdout** is approximately comparable *pre-credit, terminal downside liability shape* for a one-short/one-long put vertical at a declared expiration. The shape includes where the short leg begins to lose, where long-leg protection caps the loss, and the transition between them. This makes the complete spread, not its short leg, the sampling unit for later Exit Reliability comparisons. It is a target hypothesis, **not** an adopted selector or a claim of equal economic risk.

For a verified ordinary same-deliverable class with short strike `Ks > Kl` and common authorized multiplier `m`, the gross expiration liability at underlying settlement value `p` is `G(p) = m[max(Ks−p,0) − max(Kl−p,0)]`, with cap `m(Ks−Kl)`. A price-only, option-quote-independent shape is `g(x) = G(S exp(a(T)x)) / [m(Ks−Kl)]`, where `S` is a declared reference price and `a(T)` is a **separately declared underlying-only downside shock scale** for the expiration horizon. The onset and saturation points of `g`, and the interval between them, describe both legs and width. The gross cap in currency and relative to reference underlying notional must be reported alongside the normalized shape; normalizing by the cap must not hide dollar scale. No opening credit, quote midpoint, broker margin, or observed package price enters this target.

This target is relevant because the moneyness and protection region of the *whole position* can materially affect its mark and management path. It controls a plausible source of cross-class risk variation before studying the market's ability to close the spread. Choosing an underlying-move scale is itself a risk-model assumption; an RV-scaled diagnostic would mechanically favor a selector using the same RV, so an independent diagnostic reference is necessary. This target does **not** equalize the credit received, mark-to-market Greek vector, volatility skew, gap/path or early-assignment risk, management-trigger timing, settlement mechanics, or actual closing price. Those are later evidence or explicit strata. The choice of `a(T)`, the desired shape region, acceptable shape distance, and position-size/cap treatment remain **unresolved before preregistration**; no prior-panel delta statistic supplies them. For adjusted classes or index settlement, the formula and reference mapping are withheld until authoritative contract terms establish their actual payoff.

The recorded eight-underlying comparison does not select a rule. At 50 DTE all tested coordinates matched the same eight of nine ordinary exact-root rows. RV20 narrowed the matched short-leg delta span to 0.0672 from price-relative 0.1792, while net-position spans were 0.0535 and 0.0507 respectively. Seven of eight matched pairs changed across RV20/40/60. TSLL was unmatched under every rule; at 43 DTE exact-root SPX lacked puts while SPXW exposed them. Choosing RV20 from these diagnostics would tune to this panel. Neither a hybrid nor a preferred history window has an independent justification yet.

## Candidate position-level invariants

“Independent” below means the contract **selection** can ignore bid/ask spread, displayed size, OI, volume, and broker quality labels. Observing such fields later is still necessary for market-quality and Exit Reliability research. Pass 0's adopted geometry and ancillary quote/history can replay price/RV coordinates if the reference protocol is declared; it does not certify contract terms or supply a ready-made Greek model.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Net position delta** (`long-put Δ − short-put Δ`) | Approximate *local* directional sensitivity of the complete spread. Greek values need not use liquidity fields, but provider Greeks may depend on option marks and volatility inferred from the same quote surface later judged for quality. | Exact two contracts, signed quantities, synchronized Greek values and provenance. Selecting on provider delta then testing delta dispersion is circular. Adjusted deliverables require authoritative multiplier/payoff mapping before aggregation. |

An analytically specified *position region* could combine leg locations and width with a gross-payoff bound; this is a research hypothesis, not a third adopted rule. The older [credit-spread discovery](../39-credit-spreads-deployment-behavioral-discipline-discovery.md) motivates considering distance, width, delta, and maximum loss together, while the [options-domain contract](../foundations/options-domain-competence-contract.md) prevents treating a Greek as probability or `contract_size` as complete deliverable authority.

The **selector coordinate** is the frozen rule mapping a class's strikes and ancillary reference evidence to one pair or an unmatched state. The **target** above is the economic property the research wants approximately comparable. A **diagnostic** measures what selected pairs actually exhibit. Net and leg Greeks are possible diagnostics only when their timestamps, convention and contract terms are interpretable; they cannot validate a coordinate selected by those same Greeks. Likewise, measuring a candidate's payoff curve using the very volatility estimate that selected its strikes is partly tautological. The later diagnostic must use a separately declared shock reference or report that dependence explicitly.

All candidates require an exact-class, same-expiration pair and an explicit strike-grid tolerance. A missing expiration, insufficient strikes, stale/missing reference, or impossible target produces an accountable state, not a nearest-root or nearest-date rescue. At fixed calendar DTE, expiry style and observation phase can still alter the exact-root lattice. Width and the gross cap belong in every position-level report; width by itself cannot locate the loss region.

## Review of the initial holdout sketch

The first draft was useful as an experimental outline, **not a true preregistration**. It did not define the ETF/equity/index source-metadata classifier, hash salt or row serialization, duplicate-underlying rule, numeric targets, reference freshness, or an independently justified shock scale. Its one-quarter strike tolerance was inherited from the prior pilot without a rationale tied to the position target. Requiring *zero* unmatched classes for “universal” coverage overstated what a small sample can test. A changed contract pair across history windows is evidence of selector instability, but it does **not** by itself falsify risk-shape comparability: two pairs may differ while their independently evaluated liability shapes remain close. The two-date same-expiration design removes the weekly-versus-monthly expiry contrast from the previous pilot but remains confounded by elapsed time, spot moves, and market regime. None of these gaps should be filled after seeing holdout data.

## Holdout protocol to preregister **before** any new acquisition

The proposed small experiment is a **design shell, not yet authorized or executable**. Its frozen protocol must contain the following concrete choices and its exact source/code hashes before any holdout quote, history or chain is requested:

1. **Population and accounting.** Name the frozen Pass −1 manifest and keep its 6,072-underlying/6,381-class denominators. Generate holdout rows only from that OCC class table. Exclude all class rows of the prior eight underlying products (`SPY`, `QQQ`, `XSP`, `SPX`, `TSLL`, OCC `BRKB` / explicit Tradier `BRK/B`, `DRAM`, `TLT`) from *diagnostic sampling*, without deleting them from the parent denominator. Define mutually exclusive strata from frozen OCC/Cboe fields, with explicit `metadata unknown`, `numbered/potentially nonstandard`, and `identity unresolved` states. A numeric suffix is not proof of adjusted deliverables. Publish the classifier and any unmapped metadata before drawing the sample.
2. **Deterministic draw.** Freeze the salt, canonical row-byte serialization, per-stratum class counts, total class and distinct-underlying counts, and whether more than one class of an underlying may be selected. Hash-sort OCC `(underlying, class)` identities inside each stratum; specify a source-only fallback for an undersized stratum. Preserve sampled identity-unresolved and term-unverified rows as outcomes even if no selector can run. Never redraw because a chain, reference field, or Greek is missing. Current Tradier quality or the prior panel's diagnostic results may not affect sampling.
3. **Position target and candidate selectors.** Freeze the independent shock scale `a(T)`, the target downside-liability curve/region, the acceptable deviation, and how gross cap or one-lot size is compared **before** seeing holdout results. Compare only two interpretable coordinate families: (A) `ln(Kshort/S), ln(Klong/S)` and (B) `ln(Kshort/S)/(RVw sqrt(D/365)), ln(Klong/S)/(RVw sqrt(D/365))`. In B, `RVw` is `sqrt(252)` times sample standard deviation of the last `w` valid close-to-close log returns. Use **20 and 60 returns as predeclared sensitivity factors**, neither designated the winner; 40 may be omitted to keep the experiment small. The exact two-leg targets and any width/cap constraint remain unset pending an independent economic rationale. Do not import 95%/90%, `−0.5/−1.0`, or another hybrid simply because of prior delta spans.
4. **Reference and contract evidence.** Freeze the Tradier quote field used as `S`, quote/provider timestamps, age and chain-separation ceilings, historical source/version, split/dividend adjustment convention, bar calendar and gap treatment, corporate-action handling, alignment of historical-price units with `S`, and the number of bars needed for both selector windows **and** an independent diagnostic shock reference. Keep the raw quote/history and hashes linked to the exact class root. Declare the source/version and effective date of authoritative multiplier, deliverable and settlement terms. If missing, record `reference insufficient` or `terms unverified` separately from Pass 0 geometry; do not substitute an underlying or infer terms from `contract_size`.
5. **Expiration and matching.** Fix one named standard monthly calendar expiration and two observation dates before acquisition, nominally around 50 and 43 calendar DTE *for the same expiration*. Use only its observed exact OCC/Tradier root; preserve any root-specific weekly/monthly or settlement distinction. Declare target-strike conversion, leg-by-leg coordinate distance, tie-break, `Kshort > Klong`, an economically justified tolerance, and whether width is implied by two leg targets or separately constrained. With no compliant pair, report unmatched; no delta, neighboring expiry, alternate class, or nearest-root fallback. A failed request is an acquisition state, not an unmatched finding. This paired observation tests sensitivity, not a causal phase effect.
6. **Diagnostics and falsifiers.** On the complete sampled denominator, report identity, acquisition, reference, terms, pair match and unmatched reasons by stratum/date/family/window. For verified matched positions, report exact contract IDs; both leg coordinates; width and gross cap in currency and relative to underlying notional; the full gross-liability response on a **separately declared** downside shock grid; short/long and signed-net Greeks only when freshness and convention are established; and pair and *risk-shape* changes across windows and dates. Keep Greek unavailability visible. Publish before capture the coverage and shape-deviation thresholds that would count against each candidate, plus the separate contract-instability threshold. A missing pair tests coverage; a changed pair tests identity stability; only an independently measured shape miss tests the position-risk target. Report all diagnostics, not just whichever span is smallest. No formula, threshold, stratum, date, or exclusion changes after results are visible.

This holdout could reject a candidate's stated **coverage** or **pre-credit shape-comparability** claim under its declared shock model, or reveal reference/contract-term insufficiency. It cannot establish a universally best coordinate, equal true loss probability, equal mark-to-market or management-trigger risk, equal execution quality, or longitudinal Exit Reliability. Even a successful pilot would require replication and a separate Principal decision before a Pass 1 method is adopted. **The blocking preregistration choices today are the independent shock scale/target region, numeric acceptable shape and coverage tolerances, contract/cap comparability convention, exact source-only strata/sample, and reference freshness/adjustment protocol.** Do not acquire a holdout until these are frozen.

## Sufficiency and handoff boundary

The adopted Pass 0 ancillary quote/history contract is **sufficient in kind** to replay price-relative and historical-RV coordinates for exact-root ordinary classes, provided the future protocol declares a long enough history horizon, price adjustment, timestamp/freshness, source/version, bar gaps, and exact reference-price field **before** capture. It is not yet a guarantee that those fields have been captured or interpreted. An independently computed scenario return distribution needs the same history provenance; a risk-neutral probability or broker-capital selector would need additional evidence and would change the research question.

For **payoff or capital-at-risk claims**, the genuinely missing independent evidence is authoritative class-level contract terms: deliverable composition and cash components, multiplier, settlement/exercise convention, effective date and source version, joined to exact OCC/Tradier contract identity. Tradier `contract_size: 100` alone did not resolve `TSLL1` in the prior pilot. Keep term-unknown classes in the denominator with a separate state. Do not change Pass 0 geometry state, launch its full crawl, or choose a Pass 1 rule to fill this gap.
