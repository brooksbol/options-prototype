## Completed Principal walkthrough — working directions

1. **Construction Model versus Construction instance/value.** The Principal questions orphaned durable Constructions: instances convey contextual, temporal meaning, particularly when associated with Position. Reusable, versioned Construction Models behave like flyweights. Prospective option-search candidates can be passed as typed JSON/value representations under a reusable OAS schema, **without mandatory URI, GUID, durability, or Position association**. A Position-linked persistent Construction remains as accepted; a schema/value does not become a brokerage-established Position, Order or execution. Whether other enduring contexts should ever own a Construction remains open, with no pressure to decide for this slice.
2. **Chains and evidence.** "This is not a new problem. We work with the data we have." Reuse existing Evidence Appliance qualification and retention; evaluate the observed/eligible universe rather than requiring proof of complete market coverage. Avoid creating new disclaimer machinery. Market closure is not a blocker: use latest available qualified open-session evidence on weekends, holidays and after hours; don't claim executable current pricing or invent data. No new Chain ontology decision was required in this walkthrough. `/v2/chains/SPY` remains an optional supporting resource candidate, not the center of the Product.
3. **Backend search.** API v2 backend owns enumeration of compatible pairs, evidence admission/exclusion, payoff economics, supported metrics, ranking and multiobjective leaders. MCP carries conversational intent and presents backend-authoritative outputs, without sourcing chains or performing independent financial judgment.
4. **Progressive refinement.** "almost like a sorting algorithm. divide and conquer. zero in on the right answer." Qualify ahead of time (underlying, DTE, widths etc.), evaluate a bounded universe, then conversationally tighten/relax hard constraints or reorder by ranking preference. Several Pareto/criterion leaders can remain; no universal "best" score or implicit 45 DTE. Compare distinct constructions, not duplicate leader cards. **Narrowing funnel with counts at each stage** explains candidate elimination; **compact comparison table** shows what survived and why. Example counts are illustrative, not evidence. This visual pair received positive Principal feedback.
5. **Conversational criteria and Regime.** All filters and preferences derive from conversational intent, with backend validation and existing applicable governance. Operating Regime is simply a specialization of this qualification: when an explicitly governed capital/inventory scope supplies it, apply its rules; do not infer a regime from Account or ticker. Don't create an alternative regime-search engine.
6. **Saved searches and forms.** After refining search and result presentation, operator may choose to save the search definition (parameters, constraints, ranking preferences) and preferred answer form (funnel, compact table, columns, formatting) under the already deferred *My Questions* Talk concept in `PL-MCP-01`. On reuse, re-evaluate with the relevant market evidence; do not freeze prior winners. Saving is explicit, not mandatory.
7. **Ephemeral by default.** "i don't see the point of persisting moment in time searches. maybe something will emerge but until then..." Ordinary searches, per-candidate construction values and momentary results do not create durable search history or a registry. Existing market-evidence retention persists under existing authority. Temporary state needed to support an in-flight HTTP operation is not an enduring Product history. Search definition/form persistence only upon operator save (future feature).
8. **Acquisition and progressively useful UI.** Reuse backend acquisition when actually needed and authorized; data arrives incrementally. Principal wants substantive candidates/progress during natural waiting rather than a blinking cursor, **unless substantial implementation/maintenance complexity makes it unwarranted**. Favor conventional HTTP (collections, 202 Accepted, status/next hyperlinks, polling/conditional GET if justified) over a novel streaming subsystem. Distinguish provisional from complete results and do not confuse pagination with waiting. MCP can map HTTP workflow to conversational updates if supported; otherwise give progress/completed result, not fictitious streaming. Backend owns the actual search state, evidence and financial conclusions.
9. **Presentation/attention.** Maintain operator focus; don't bury results under qualification text. If real progress/results exist, they take priority over ambient capability tips. Refine the Product through early observable specimens rather than extensive speculative apparatus.
