## `PL-BETA-01` — Private Beta / Trusted Evaluator Program

**Date:** September 24, 2026  
**State:** RECONCILED intake — Principal-directed durable capture of the trusted-evaluator operating model; no private-beta agreement is yet lawyer-reviewed and no multi-user implementation is authorized by this record  
**Why-state:** `docs/62-private-beta-trusted-evaluator-discovery-2026-09-24.md`

### Intake

The Principal intends to allow a very small number of specifically trusted friends/family with varied financial/trading experience to use Wheelwright against their own portfolios and provide Product feedback.

Initial real evaluator specimens discussed are:

- Jeff — CFO / high financial knowledge, not specifically options-experienced;
- Daniel — trader in another market (Pokémon cards), not options-experienced.

The desired relationship is a **closed, invitation-only, no-charge private beta / trusted evaluator program**, not a public product launch, advisory service, brokerage service, managed-account arrangement, or commercial SaaS offering.

Canonical operating loop:

> **invite + affirmative acceptance → evaluator account → evaluator-controlled Fidelity CSV import → Wheelwright analysis → evaluator independently decides/executes → bugs/questions/feature requests/usability feedback → Product learning**

Evaluators may report bugs, request enhancements, ask questions, challenge recommendations, and expose confusing/missing Product behavior. The Principal may explain Wheelwright's calculations, policy, terminology, and software-generated results. That support/feedback loop is part of the intended evaluation model.

### Decision / execution boundary

The intended boundary is:

> **Wheelwright analyzes. The evaluator decides and executes.**

Private-beta users control their own brokerage accounts and investment decisions. Wheelwright remains decision-support software and does not execute trades. The Principal does not receive custody or trading authority and is not undertaking to manage evaluator portfolios or determine personal suitability.

The Product may truthfully use **recommendation** language. The relevant distinction is that a Wheelwright recommendation is generated by governed software policy; the private-beta relationship is not represented as the Principal personally determining that a transaction is suitable for the evaluator.

### Compensation / entity boundary

Current Principal intent is no charge, subscription, advisory fee, profit share, referral/brokerage compensation, or other economic benefit tied to evaluator trading. There is no present plan to commercialize Wheelwright.

The Principal may use Wheelwright internally to manage capital owned by an existing portfolio-management LLC. That internal tool use is distinct from Wheelwright being a business/product/service/DBA/advisory activity of the LLC. Existing portfolio LLCs are not intended to provide the private beta, and no Wheelwright LLC is planned.

These are factual/Product boundaries, not legal conclusions.

### Approved private-beta intake protocol

For the initial tiny cohort, onboarding is deliberately manual:

1. **Invite.** Principal sends an account-creation/private-beta invitation email.
2. **Disclose.** Email identifies Wheelwright as experimental private-beta software supplied without charge for testing/evaluation/feedback and includes or links the current **Wheelwright Private Beta Software Evaluation Agreement**, identified by version.
3. **Affirmative opt-in.** Invitee replies affirmatively that they reviewed and accept that agreement version and want to participate.
4. **Provision after acceptance.** Principal creates/activates the evaluator account only after receiving the acceptance.
5. **Retain the paper trail.** Preserve the invitation and affirmative reply as the acceptance record.

At the initial scale (approximately two evaluators), the email exchange is intentionally sufficient as the acceptance mechanism. A click-through/automated acceptance subsystem is not required merely to begin the beta. If scale later makes manual administration burdensome, automation can enter normal intake/design separately.

The agreement/disclosure must describe actual Product behavior rather than create a contradictory fiction. Candidate agreement subjects and change triggers are preserved in Doc 62. Exact legal language is not ratified by this Product record and should receive appropriate legal review before being treated as legally sufficient.

### Product-learning rationale

Trusted evaluators with different backgrounds create evidence the Principal cannot obtain solely by operating his own software:

- options-semantic comprehension;
- transfer of trading intuition from other domains;
- Explanation quality;
- recommendation legibility;
- real Fidelity import/account specimens;
- missing operator jobs;
- bugs and usability failures;
- feature demand.

Do not over-coach evaluators merely to make the Product appear understandable. Where safe use permits, evaluator confusion is useful Product evidence.

## `PL-OPS-CSV-01` — Operator CSV-upload burden (authoritative ownership now costs a fourth manual export)

**Date:** September 24, 2026  
**State:** RECONCILED intake — Principal operator-experience signal captured during BUG-026 acceptance; no workflow redesign or implementation authorized by this record.  
**Why-state:** `docs/bugs/BUG-026-derive-inventory-collapses-additive-share-lots.md` (Principal acceptance — 2026-09-24); ADR-020 (`docs/07c-adrs.md`).

### Intake

ADR-020 made the Fidelity **Positions** export the authoritative source of aggregate share ownership, resolving BUG-026 (false over-encumbrance on genuinely-additive covered-call lots). During acceptance the Principal observed that this adds a **fourth** manual CSV per portfolio refresh (Option Summary + Balances + Activity + Positions), i.e. more operator work each cycle.

This is an operator-experience/product-workflow cost, **not** a correctness defect. ADR-020 deliberately makes Positions authoritative only **when supplied** (absent Positions, the conservative observed value is preserved), so the burden is opt-in — but for any multi-lot covered position that needs correct ownership, Positions becomes required in practice, which is the friction observed.

### Concern

The manual evidence-refresh workflow grows with each authoritative source. Four separate Fidelity exports per refresh is a real recurring operator cost and a candidate source of stale/mismatched evidence (an operator may refresh some CSVs and not others).

### Candidate directions (not authorized; for later reconciliation)

- Whether Positions could **substitute for** rather than **add to** an existing upload — e.g. sourcing aggregate ownership (and possibly share basis) from Positions so it partially replaces the Option Summary's role, reducing the count rather than increasing it.
- Whether a single combined export or a guided multi-file upload could reduce per-file operator steps.
- Whether the eventual broker-API / Evidence-Appliance direction collapses the manual CSV set entirely, making this burden transitional.
- Operator-facing freshness/coherence signalling when the four exports are taken at materially different times (relates to the checkpoint seam behind BUG-023).

### Relationship to existing concerns

- **ADR-020** — the ratified ownership-authority decision that introduced the fourth upload; this item does not reopen that decision, only its workflow cost.
- **`PL-PORT-01`** — portfolio-state / evidence-import semantics; the upload workflow and evidence set live in that neighborhood. This item is the operator-cost lens, not a redefinition of portfolio semantics.
- **BUG-023** — multi-export temporal reconciliation (checkpoint vs the evidence being mutated); more exports taken at different times increases the surface this seam must handle.
- **Evidence Appliance / cloud direction** — the end-state may remove manual CSV refresh; this item is explicitly a current-workflow concern, not a challenge to system identity.

### Reconciliation Completion Record

- **Intake:** new canonical identity **`PL-OPS-CSV-01`**. Parking-lot reconciliation found portfolio-import (`PL-PORT-01`) and temporal-reconciliation (BUG-023) neighbors, but no existing identity owning the **operator per-refresh CSV-count burden** as a first-class Product-experience concern.
- **Strategic disposition:** **no new LVT Bet and no `docs/roadmap.md` change.** This is operator-experience friction on an existing capability, not a new strategic destination.
- **Architectural disposition:** **no `docs/architecture-roadmap.md` change.** Reducing upload burden is a workflow/product-surface concern; any evidence-source substitution that touched ownership authority would return through ADR-level reconciliation.
- **Parking-lot disposition/mapping:** **retained as `PL-OPS-CSV-01`**, cross-linked to `PL-PORT-01`, BUG-023, and ADR-020; not double-booked as any of them.
- **Next authorized mode:** exploration/design only when deliberately selected by the Principal. No implementation, upload-workflow redesign, or ownership-source substitution is authorized by this record.

### Explicitly not authorized

This record does not authorize changing ADR-020's ownership authority, making Positions mandatory, altering the upload surface, sourcing ownership/basis from a different export, or any evidence-substitution that would change which document is authoritative for a fact.
