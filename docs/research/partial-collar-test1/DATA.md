# Data provenance and limitations

Main dataset: Yahoo IWM daily history, plus Cboe RVX via FRED (`RVXCLS`) with Cboe's direct file as a corroborating source. Yahoo OHLC is split-adjusted; adjusted close additionally accounts for distributions. [Yahoo adjustment explanation](https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN28256.html). [iShares IWM](https://www.ishares.com/us/products/239710/ishares-russell-2000-etf). [FRED RVX](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/RVXCLS). [Cboe RVX file](https://cdn.cboe.com/api/global/us_indices/daily_prices/RVX_History.csv).

IWM capture covers 2000-05-26 through 2026-10-07, 6, 630 sessions. RVX starts 2004-01-02. Simulation starts 2005-06-10 after IWM's 2: 1 split 2005-06-09; starts after indicator warmup. [SEC corroboration of June2005 split](https://www.sec.gov/file/34-56090). Raw vendor split-adjusted OHLC and distributions are converted to actual contemporary prices by undoing **future** splits. The dated split is retained in normalized records; pre-split pricing is not used to purchase fictional low-priced 100-share sleeves. A sleeve-resizing policy is needed to simulate crossing the split, so the main experiment uses the longest post-split unchanged 100-share interval, not all available history.

No missing or off-calendar IWM sessions under XNYS. Calendar dependency predates the 2025-01-09 Carter national day of mourning, explicitly removed as a documented special closure. RVX missing on 2005-10-10 and 2005-11-11 remains missing; confirmed fast triggers/reference computations are disabled where requisite values are absent. No forward fill or interpolation. FRED/Cboe overlap 4, 286 observations with zero closing-value discrepancy. All 107 vendor distribution events retained; causal versus vendor adjustment-factor changes agree within 1.26 e-6. This validates arithmetic consistency, not independent issuer auditing of every distribution or proof against vendor revisions.

Signals use a causal dividend-neutralized price index, updated with distributions known on their ex-dates and with the split ratio on its effective date. Prices for trades remain actual OHLC. Back-adjusted OHLC is persisted for reference only. Yahoo timestamps converted UTC -> America/New_York session date. RVX represents Cboe daily close, potentially later than IWM's regular-session close. The after-close decision is assumed to have both observations available before the next 9: 30 open. Archived data is a current vintage: historical publication latency/revisions are not recovered. No trade uses that same day's close as a fill.

Dividends are receivables on ex-date for shares carried through the prior close, included in NAV once and not immediately deployable. Actual historical payment dates unavailable in the Yahoo event feed; baseline assumes seven-session delay, with 0/14-day sensitivities. No automatic reinvestment. Settled cash only; equity sale settlement T+2 until 2024-05-28 and T+1 thereafter. Constant 0/2/4% cash yields are assumptions, not historical cash-rate observations. Taxes excluded. Volume retained in source-vendor units, not used in signals/fill-capacity calculations. No survivorship-selected universe: one Principal-specified ETF; no claim that another underlying would behave similarly.

## ThetaData

User downloaded `ThetaTerminalv3.jar` and created a local credentials file; authentication confirmed by actual successful historical requests. No credentials are read, echoed, copied into this project or passed by the data scripts. Current docs support terminal email/password and (newer versions) API-key authentication. Our actual session used the user-created email/password file. [ThetaData setup](https://thetadata.net/docs/Articles/Getting-Started/Getting-Started.html).

Empirical probes: IWM 2004/2008/2020 and RVX 2020 returned 403; IWMJune 2023 and September 2026, RVXSeptember 2026 returned 200 with records. RVX is listed among terminal index symbols. Access 2023 does not prove any undocumented earlier entitlement. This cannot cover 2008/2020 stress tests; no paid subscription purchased or account changed.

`theta_probe.py` persists coverage evidence; `theta_download.py` requests monthly IWM/RVX EOD files 2023-06 through 2026-10-07 and reports observed coverage and cross-provider close differences, including any unavailable months. It is independent corroboration, not spliced into older primary series. Theta EOD reports are produced around 17: 15 ET; last_trade can be after the equity regular close, RVX near 16: 15. [Theta EOD semantics](https://thetadata.net/docs/operations/stock_history_eod.html). Matching sampled close values does not establish equal session filtering or quote-time availability. Preserve this timing difference.

Raw captures and normalized CSVs are local and ignored by Git; public reproducibility/source/hash manifests and model outputs are versioned. FRED states RVX is copyrighted by Cboe; cite as Chicago Board Options Exchange, CBOE Russell 2000 Volatility Index [RVXCLS], retrieved from FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. Further redistribution requires respecting source terms. Limitations may qualify a pass; they must not be used to erase an observed model failure.
