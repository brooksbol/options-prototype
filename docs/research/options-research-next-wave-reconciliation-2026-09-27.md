# Reconciled Next Research Wave — Sustainable Options Operation

**Date:** 2026-09-27  
**Basis:** `docs/research/options-research-program-reorientation-memo-2026-09-27.md` and `docs/research/options-research-program-reorientation-analysis-2026-09-27.md`  
**Status:** Principal reconciliation of research priority and scope. This document authorizes no empirical investigation by itself.

## 1. Purpose

The next research wave is governed by one priority function:

> Reduce uncertainty about mechanisms that can destroy the capital base, remove operator control, conceal bad economics, or make a repeatable operating process unsustainable.

Scientific interest, parameter precision, and completeness are subordinate to operator consequence.

Evidence strength and research priority remain separate dimensions. A weakly evidenced capital-threatening question may outrank a strongly evidenced marginal question, but weak evidence never becomes a prescription.

## 2. Epistemic boundary

The reorientation memo is WW-directed synthesis, not WW-sanitized independent domain research. Its prioritization is conditioned on Principal/Wheelwright objectives.

Future domain-research assignments in this wave must restore the sanitation boundary:

> options domain → independent external research → durable evidence → later Wheelwright reconciliation.

Do not expose Wheelwright file paths, ADRs, ontology, implementation state, Product requirements, internal process identifiers, or desired conclusions to domain researchers.

Principal-specific operating facts may determine applicability and priority, but they are not external empirical evidence about options traders generally.

Principal operating records, if later used, are operator-specific observational evidence only.

## 3. Reconciled failure model

Do not universalize forced liquidation as “the ruin object.”

Maintain at least three distinct failure objects:

1. **Economic ruin** — NAV impairment makes the operation nonviable.
2. **Liquidity/collateral ruin** — the account cannot finance the position through its path even if terminal economics are bounded or potentially recoverable.
3. **Broker-enforced ruin** — broker requirements or liquidation powers remove the operator's disposition authority at an adverse time.

The load-bearing research insight is:

> **Path survivability is distinct from terminal payoff. An operator must be able to continue owning the economic position through plausible stress without involuntarily losing disposition authority.**

The importance and frequency of each failure object may vary materially by structure, account regime, leverage, broker, and portfolio aggregation.

## 4. Wave questions

### R1 — Loss-of-disposition failure boundary

**Question**

Under relevant options structures and brokerage/account regimes, what conditions can cause an operator to lose disposition authority through collateral, margin, assignment, expiration, or broker liquidation constraints, and what observable preconditions exist before that happens?

**Why first**

This asks directly how a process can cease to be operator-controlled. It avoids the unavailable population denominator in the prior forced-liquidation-incidence proposal.

**Required distinctions**

- cash-secured versus leveraged;
- defined-risk versus undefined-risk;
- Reg-T versus portfolio margin where evidence permits;
- single-position versus simultaneous portfolio stress;
- economic loss versus collateral insufficiency versus broker liquidation;
- market-driven requirement expansion versus broker house-margin change.

**Falsifier / useful null**

If relevant fully funded or bounded structures preserve disposition authority across the tested stress conditions, record that boundary. Do not generalize leveraged-book failure mechanisms to them.

**Kill condition**

If primary mechanics and bounded stress evidence establish the failure boundaries but population incidence remains unavailable, stop. Do not reopen a search for a universal failure-rate denominator.

**Output**

A domain evidence map of failure conditions, observables, transfer boundaries, and unresolved cells. No trading rule or capital threshold.

---

### R2 — Capital adequacy and stress survivability

**Question**

How do exposure, uncommitted capital, simultaneous adverse movement, volatility expansion, assignment obligations, and margin requirements interact to determine whether an account can continue carrying its positions through stress?

**Why second**

The verified mechanism behind reserve folklore is important, but “reserve percentage” may merely be another representation of sizing. Research the economic object rather than presupposing a 40–50% reserve rule.

**Do not optimize**

Do not ask “what is the optimal reserve percentage?”

Potential representations—reserve fraction, utilization ceiling, stress collateral coverage, aggregate exposure limit—are hypotheses, not predefined answers.

**Falsifier / useful null**

If reserve has no independent survival effect after exposure/sizing is held economically equivalent, record the null and retire reserve-as-independent-defense.

**Kill condition**

Stop once the evidence can distinguish or cannot distinguish reserve from economically equivalent sizing. No parameter sweep for a best percentage.

**Output**

Evidence on capital-adequacy mechanisms and measurable quantities, with explicit unknowns. No prescribed utilization or reserve number.

---

### R3 — Bounded sequential funded-account accounting

**Question**

For one explicitly specified, operator-relevant options Operating Program, what happens when it is accounted for sequentially at funded-account level, including all specified frictions, collateral/margin events, assignment/exercise treatment, overlapping positions, and geometric account evolution?

**Why third**

Trade-level averages, premium collected, win rate, adjusted cost basis, and arithmetic extrapolation do not answer whether a funded operation survives.

**Selection prerequisite**

The tested Program must be relevant to an actual operator decision. Do not select the historical 45/50/21 composite merely because prior research already exists.

Before execution, freeze the Program specification and explain why that specimen is operator-relevant.

**Preregistration minimum**

- underlying/population;
- entry and exit;
- quantity/sizing;
- collateral and account regime;
- overlap/cadence;
- assignment/exercise;
- dividends/corporate actions where applicable;
- commissions/fees;
- spread/slippage treatment with explicit Q0 limitations;
- margin/liquidation treatment;
- account accounting;
- benchmark;
- success/failure measures;
- missing-data behavior;
- kill condition.

**Falsifier / useful null**

Non-survival is a result. Inability to specify the Program without unresolved load-bearing assumptions is also a result.

**Kill condition**

One preregistered attempt. No second specification, parameter search, rescue optimization, or backtest-of-the-month cascade.

**Output**

One bounded funded-account result and its transfer limits. No universal strategy conclusion.

---

### R4 — Revision governance

**Question**

Is there any validated, operational, non-outcome-only method for distinguishing “the operating program is structurally broken” from “the current trade/period is painful variance” before an override, abandonment, or revision decision?

**Why fourth**

A repeatable process faces symmetric hazards: emotional abandonment and mechanical persistence in a genuinely invalid program.

**Research scope**

Timeboxed search across relevant empirical decision research, quantitative model governance, documented fund shutdown/revision practices, contemporaneous postmortems, and other independent evidence lineages.

Do not treat practitioner aphorisms as validated methods.

**Falsifier / useful null**

A bounded search that finds no validated adjudication method is a useful terminal finding.

**Kill condition**

The predefined evidence/timebox expires. Declare ABSENCE-OF-EVIDENCE and stop; do not extend through “one more source set.”

**Output**

Validated methods if any; otherwise explicit unresolved governance premises. No automatic Wheelwright revision policy.

## 6. Brokerage research rule

Brokerage is researched as **process capability plus operator-control boundary**, not as a generic broker ranking.

Two sides remain distinct:

**Capabilities available to the operator:** required structures/order types, lifecycle handling, assignment/expiration support, risk visibility, APIs/automation where the process actually needs them.

**Powers retained by the broker:** margin methodology, house overlays, concentration requirements, liquidation discretion, notification behavior, assignment/expiration processing, and operational resilience.

Precise price-improvement differences remain micro unless evidence shows material impact on survival or the economics of the relevant Program.

## 7. Macro/micro interpretation

Use macro/micro only as a provisional consequence classifier.

**Macro:** being wrong can materially threaten capital, portfolio control, or viability/repeatability of the operation.

**Micro:** being wrong primarily reduces efficiency of an otherwise viable operation.

The classification does not establish causal truth. Evidence can promote or demote an issue.

## 8. Wave-wide stopping rules

Every question must pass all of these before execution:

1. **Operator-decision test:** identify the behavior, risk boundary, capability decision, or survival uncertainty the answer changes.
2. **Falsifier first:** state evidence that would overturn the current belief.
3. **Bounded scope:** define a kill condition before searching.
4. **Terminal absence:** after the bound is exhausted, ABSENCE-OF-EVIDENCE is a result.
5. **Nulls count:** a null, contradiction dissolution, or non-survival retires questions.
6. **No parameter mining:** do not convert a survival question into optimization.
7. **No silent population transfer:** structure/account/broker/population boundaries travel with evidence.
8. **No research-to-policy conversion:** findings do not become trading rules, Product requirements, architecture, implementation, or Operating Programs without later Principal reconciliation.
9. **Survivorship audit:** survivor-derived lessons carry an explicit missing-population caveat.
10. **Independence discipline:** shared practitioner lineage is not independent replication.
11. **One question does not cascade automatically into the next:** completion returns to reconciliation before another empirical question begins.
