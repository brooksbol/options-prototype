## Conclusions that need correction

1. **No published literature** cannot be certified by a finite search. The located peer-reviewed condor study is a direct counterexample to the broad absence claim. A narrower claim about the exact US ETF/tenor/target policy remains unresolved. Literature coverage is not a prerequisite for testing a mechanically defined payoff on valid data.
2. **Direct regression is a plausible low-complexity method, not an established solution for this target.** Papers on premium-normalized, delta-hedged monthly returns do not directly verify dollar P/L for four-leg periodically managed positions. Cross-sectional Fama–MacBeth inference is not automatically suitable for a small correlated ETF time panel. Preserve the estimator/target/population distinction and existing chronological/block-inference requirements.
3. **Tail measures do not require a full density.** Quantiles or expected shortfall can be estimated directly, subject to appropriate data and separate validation. Improving mean forecasts does not establish their accuracy. The report's assertion that full-distribution methods are necessary for tails conflicts with its own quantile-regression entry.
4. **A price touch or trade-through does not certify our package-order fill.** Trade prints, quoted sizes and delayed observations can define scenarios; they do not reveal queue priority, order eligibility or simultaneous complex execution. Treat midpoint/quoted-side/delay variants as named assumptions, not calibrated best/worst bounds. Additional depth is useful evidence, not an automatic prerequisite for every explicitly qualified quote-path simulation.
5. **Forecast and economic falsifiers must stay separate.** Negative realized strategy P/L does not reject accurate forecasts; correctly identifying losing opportunities may have value. OOS point-error ordering needs uncertainty and relevance tests. Five tail events is not a justified universal cutoff, and a two-percent volatility perturbation needs units and source relevance. No such scalars are adopted as evidence-based thresholds.
6. **SPX is a candidate alternative, not a demonstrated best first instrument.** The repository's [parked Q0 disposition](../options-trading-research-state-transition-q0-rabbit-hole-parked-2026-09-27.md) already records unresolved retail SPX 30–60 DTE execution calibration. That is a local dependency, not a universal prohibition, but it does not disappear by switching away from ETF exercise complexity. Historical entitlement, sample coverage, settlement conventions and comparable costs remain to be verified.
7. **Independence and completeness remain unestablished.** Separate authors/publications may reuse OptionMetrics and overlapping periods. The second report's broad pre-2010 characterization must be reconciled with the directly checked 1996–2022 paper from round one. Source abstracts can verify their explicit statements, not every proposed empirical generalization.

## Applied changes and unresolved scope

Draft protocol 0.2 adds an RV-only predictor with matched non-volatility features, compares B2 primarily against that historical-information baseline, and retains the IV-only comparison as secondary. This is a basic ablation justified by the question itself, not acceptance of the unverified Lof replication claim. No full-strip SVIX/utility-bound machinery is introduced as a mandatory baseline.

The draft also states explicitly that forecast failure, economic failure and tail uncertainty have distinct dispositions; avoids certifying borrowed cost discounts; and preserves SPY iron condors as the candidate population until actual data burden is known. It does not switch to SPX, introduce a full physical density, fit models or run outcomes. Portfolio/mean/tail observations remain distinct from alpha.

The first useful empirical artifact remains a historical contract/quote coverage specimen, not another broad literature survey. Current options access is still unverified from the audit; this report supplies no new account evidence. Once the retrieval envelope and terminal access are established, compare actual coverage burden before selecting a simpler structure. Experimental execution remains uncommissioned, and lifecycle HOLD/CLOSE/ROLL/HEDGE remains a separate scope from entry-under-fixed-exits.
