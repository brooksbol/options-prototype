Research snapshot
15 October 2026

SPX primary · SPY/XSP secondary
Platform evaluation · prospective options paper trading

The simulator is part of the experiment.

For defined-risk SPX spreads, a paper fill is evidence only when the platform preserves the order, exposes the execution rule, and lets the result be independently reconstructed.

No single platform clears every requirement. Use an API-first primary venue, an execution-fidelity cross-check, and a manual index-options check rather than treating one paper account as ground truth.

01 / EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Three leaders, three different jobs.

Alpaca is the strongest programmable research environment; IBKR is the most documented brokerage-native baseline; Tradier has the cleanest native API lifecycle. None has proven realistic SPX package fills and complete simulated fee accounting.

01
Primary programmatic venue

Alpaca

  • Free paper accounts; REST + WebSocket records
  • Atomic 2–4 leg mleg orders
  • SPX and XSP in paper and live
  • Honest, explicit simulation limits
Critical caveat
No index market-data feed yet; regulatory fees are not modeled; expiration settlement is unknown.
02
Execution-fidelity baseline

Interactive Brokers

  • Real prices and sizes; top-of-book rule documented
  • Real margin model and daily statements
  • Paper account API access
  • $1M starting equity and reset support
Critical caveat
“Limited combo trading”; third-party measurement reports synthetic midpoint fills for limits.
03
Lifecycle/API benchmark

Tradier

  • Full trading API with paper money
  • 2–4 leg credit/debit/even combos
  • GTC plus OCO/OTO/OTOCO brackets
  • Preview returns fees and margin impact
Critical caveat
The sandbox fill-price rule is undocumented; XSP acceptance and persistence guarantees are unknown.
The central limitation

Current quotes do not make a simulator realistic. Package-level fill semantics, queue position, spread crossing, size, and fees remain unverified at every leading venue.

02 / BROKERAGE-NATIVE

Practice account, sandbox, or no simulator?

Those categories are not interchangeable. Expand each platform for what the evidence actually supports.

Interactive BrokersGenuine brokerage-native · strongest documentation

Verified

  • Most order types; all TWS instruments.
  • Real prices and sizes; top-of-book only.
  • Real margin model, persistent paper account, daily statements.
  • TWS and Client Portal APIs target paper accounts.

Limits

  • Limited combo trading.
  • No deep book; US-option penny trading unsupported.
  • Stops and complex orders remain simulated.

Unknown / claimed

  • Package-level SPX vertical fills: unknown.
  • Real commission schedule in paper: third-party claim only.
  • Midpoint limit fills: third-party measurement.
WebullGenuine brokerage-native · best manual SPX/XSP practice

Verified

  • Paper options mirror all live strategies.
  • Index options list SPX/SPXW and XSP.
  • Level 3 includes credit spreads and iron condors.
  • Rolling, legging, OCO/OTO/OTOCO; order-record export.

Execution rule

  • Fill uses last traded price.
  • Requires trading volume after placement.
  • No volume means no fill.

Limits

  • No public trading API.
  • One paper account; reset erases positions, orders and history.
  • GTC and simulated fees unknown.

03 / THIRD-PARTY

One qualifying API-first alternative.

Alpaca is the only additional candidate in this evidence set that established meaningful forward options paper trading for the target instruments.

Alpaca

Verified

Free paper trading uses the same API specification as live with separate keys and hosts. Index-option access includes SPX, SPXW and XSP; 2–4-leg mleg orders are atomic. REST and WebSocket outputs can preserve order, fill, position and activity evidence.

Its candor is a strength: the vendor says paper does not model market impact, latency slippage, order queue position or regulatory fees. A completed order demonstrates workflow, not a likely live fill.

Blocking dependency

Alpaca does not yet provide index market data. An SPX experiment therefore needs a separate quote source, synchronized timestamps, and a pre-registered reconciliation rule.

Qualifying test

Persistent state
Paper account and machine-readable positions: yes.
Ongoing market orders
Simulated, not routed. Execution fidelity remains limited.
Target instruments
SPX, SPY and XSP verified.
Combination orders
Atomic 2–4 leg mleg.
Evidence export
REST/WebSocket orders, positions and activities.

Classified, not promoted

PowerOptions

Manual position journal and tracking for “paper-trading accounts”; no simulated order execution or fills.

OptionStrat / payoff calculators

Strategy analysis and risk diagrams, not persistent simulated brokerage accounts.

QuantConnect LEAN

Paper capability exists generally, but options suitability for this SPX use case was not established in the reviewed evidence.

Contest simulators

MarketWatch, Investopedia and Wall Street Survivor did not establish multi-leg index-option support.

ETNA

B2B white-label simulator; individual-researcher access not established.

Fidelity

No paper-trading capability found. Its live fee schedule is relevant only for external reconciliation.

05 / ORDER LIFECYCLE

Can the 50% target survive overnight?

The representative workflow is a 30–60 DTE two-leg credit spread, a buy-to-close order at 50% of the opening credit, and a time exit at 21 DTE. Those are test controls, not a trade recommendation.

  1. Enter as one package

    Tradier verifies net credit/debit multileg submission. Alpaca documents atomic mleg. IBKR, Schwab, TradeStation and Webull require hands-on package-fill validation.

  2. Keep the target working

    Tradier verifies GTC plus OTOCO. IBKR supports GTC and native limits. Webull verifies conditional groups but not GTC in reviewed docs. Alpaca can manage the lifecycle by API.

  3. Close at 21 DTE

    IBKR, Tradier and Alpaca can implement a time check by API. TradeStation can use EasyLanguage automation. Schwab and Webull require manual intervention on present evidence.

  4. Roll only with an audit trail

    Webull explicitly supports rolling; IBKR and TradeStation provide native workflows; API venues can cancel/replace or submit a new package. Preserve both the original and replacement orders.

06 / PARALLEL EXPERIMENTS

Separate decision records from brokerage state.

A credible control-versus-treatment comparison needs equal opportunity sets and execution assumptions, but independent positions, balances and P/L. No platform fully verified multiple simultaneous paper portfolios.

Minimum evidentiary design

Record every eligible opportunity and the no-trade decision before the outcome; timestamp the selection rule; submit identical order types under comparable capital constraints; preserve the raw simulator output; reconcile fees separately.

07 / MINIMUM PROOF OF CONCEPT

Ten observations before trusting a result.

Run this no-real-money trial on each shortlisted venue using one small 30–45 DTE SPX or XSP put credit spread. Pass means observed behavior, not a checkbox in documentation.

Package entry

Submit two legs as one net-credit limit.

Pass · One parent order ID; legs not submitted independently.

Working-order persistence

Leave a non-marketable GTC order; sign out; return after 24 hours.

Pass · Still working and not auto-filled at mid.

Fill behavior

Move the limit to marketable; record package and leg quotes at the event.

Pass · Fill sits within the touch and matches the stated rule.

Closing combo

Place buy-to-close at 50% of opening credit.

Pass · Persists and fills or cancels as one package.

Fee accounting

Inspect open and close fills after the round trip.

Pass · Commissions and exchange/regulatory fees are itemized, or their absence is explicit.

Position persistence

Close the client and reopen in the next session.

Pass · Cash, buying power, orders and P/L are intact.

Export evidence

Download or query orders, fills and position history.

Pass · Timestamps, OCC IDs, limits, fills, fees and P/L are reconstructable.

Parallel portfolios

Create an independent second portfolio with a different exit rule.

Pass · Positions and P/L do not mix; reset is isolated.

Expiration

Hold one small ITM and one OTM index option through expiration.

Pass · Cash settlement matches the contract’s settlement value.

Fee reconciliation

Recompute costs from the current published schedule.

Pass · Simulator and reconstruction match within rounding; gaps are explicit adjustments.
Final recommendation

Use a combination, not a winner-take-all platform.

Run the primary programmable experiment at Alpaca, cross-check fill and buying-power assumptions at Interactive Brokers, and use Webull as a manual SPX/XSP mechanics check. Tradier becomes a serious alternative only after its undocumented fill rule passes the proof of concept.

  1. Primary recordAlpaca for atomic multi-leg API orders and exportable, time-stamped evidence.
  2. Fidelity checkIBKR for documented top-of-book simulation, real margin logic and daily statements.
  3. Manual checkWebull for explicit volume-gated unfilled orders, rolling and index-option workflows.

Do not use Schwab paperMoney as profitability evidence, because reported midpoint behavior is systematically favorable to short premium. Do not treat tastytrade, Fidelity, payoff calculators or historical backtesters as prospective simulators.

09 / OPEN QUESTIONS

What documentation did not establish.

Unknown is a result. These gaps must be converted into observed pass/fail checks before a platform can be an evidence source.

  1. Tradier sandbox and TradeStation SIM fill-price rules.
  2. Exchange, regulatory and clearing fees applied to simulated P/L at any venue, beyond Alpaca’s explicit omission.
  3. Official confirmation that IBKR paper statements apply the real commission schedule.
  4. Tradier and Alpaca starting balance, reset and history-retention guarantees.
  5. XSP acceptance in Tradier sandbox and TradeStation SIM.
  6. Expiration, exercise, DNE and cash-settlement behavior at every venue.
  7. Package-level versus leg-level fills at IBKR, Schwab, TradeStation and Webull.
  8. Multiple simultaneous paper portfolios at all venues.
  9. Current Tradier prices and sandbox signup requirements.
  10. Direct verification of Schwab midpoint and assignment behavior.
  11. Webull paper-options approval level and GTC support.
