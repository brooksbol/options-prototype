# Cheap market-qualification experiment — preflight

**Scope decision:** 2,128 ETF/index underlyings and 2,273 OCC classes are the **qualification population**, not a finished symbol list or a production admission list. Individual equities are out of scope. Canonical research intake: `PL-RESEARCH-01`; production strategy admission remains separate under `PL-STRAT-01`. This preflight reuses frozen observations; it does not place new provider requests, define a trading threshold, change Exit Reliability, or change Deployment.

## Existing evidence before acquisition

Run `existing_market_evidence.py --pr33-dir <PR33-result-directory>` to verify the frozen PR #33 hashes and reconcile its probe details to `class-ledger.csv`. The result is [existing-market-evidence-summary.json](existing-market-evidence-summary.json).

PR #33 observed 2,529 fixed representative expiration slots for 1,641 ordinary Cboe ETF classes on the 2026-10-01 market date. **780 slots across 524 ETFs** had all four displayed leg markets usable under PR #33's explicit quote/size/recency predicate; **256 ETFs** had two such slots. **1,117 scored ETFs had no passing slot at those fixed coordinates.** That is *not* a general rejection: the probe tested at most two particular dates and one selected four-leg shape per date. A score, including zero, is also not itself a market-usability predicate.

**632 of 2,273 class rows have no PR #33 quote probe:** BLCN/TXS (2 ordinary ETF classes lacked usable underlying price), 123 other classes under the Cboe ETF underlyings, 453 OCC-name ETF-candidate classes, and 54 index classes. Of those 632, the Pass −1 ledger already marks 40 identity unresolved and one (`SMCF`) without a listed four-leg shape on the snapshot date. That leaves **591 exact-root classes with dated listed geometry but no PR #33 quote probe**. This is an upper bound on the next first-pass quote target, before product-term and price-reference checks. It is not a mandate to quote all 591.

The local production evidence SQLite file was also inspected read-only. Its active symbol list overlaps 724 Cboe ETF underlyings, 185 OCC-name ETF candidates, and two indexes (`NDX`, `SPX`). Existing cached chains may help inspect specific candidates, but the maintained ETF/Decision universe is not the Iron Condors scope; its absence cannot reject XSP or other products. Its chain projection does not carry the complete exact-class, side-timestamp and size evidence needed for this gate. This mutable local store is therefore **supplementary context**, not a reproducible denominator or a substitute for a declared quote observation.

## Proposed bounded first market gate

1. **Resolve product and terms cheaply.** Confirm the 445 OCC-name ETF candidates and 16 OCC-only `IU` index candidates by source-backed product/contract identity; preserve `unresolved`. Distinguish ordinary versus adjusted option classes, multiplier, payoff, settlement and broker support. The 267 product-type-unresolved OCC underlyings remain a separate discovery queue, not silent exclusions or automatic quote requests.
2. **Use existing class geometry.** For each exact root, prefer a plausible expiration in the declared 21–100 DTE observation window and sufficient strikes on both sides. A 40–50 DTE miss is not a structural veto. A class with no current suitable date is `dated_no_target_expiration`, not permanently non-optionable. Recheck an archived contract's identity only where a quote request fails or an observation is contradictory; do not crawl every chain first.
3. **Probe one four-leg structure first.** After a usable, timestamped underlying/index reference is obtained, select four ordered contracts by a predeclared price/geometry-only rule, independent of spreads, OI or volume. Request all four contracts in **one bulk response**, retaining the raw body, request time, exact root, contract identifiers and hash. If the first structure lacks usable markets, try one predeclared alternative expiration or geometry *before* calling it probe-failed. Do not optimize strikes after seeing quote quality.
4. **Classify market evidence.** Record per-leg positive, uncrossed bid/ask, displayed sizes, side timestamps/age, and missingness; record coarse OI/volume and spread-to-width diagnostics. A full four-leg two-sided observation is `observed_market_candidate`. Stale, zero, crossed, missing, partial, and acquisition failures remain separate states. Publish counts under more than one declared coarse activity/spread band before adopting a cutoff. None of these observations is a complex-order fill.
5. **Advance selectively.** Only classes with valid terms, reference price, geometry and a four-leg market observation enter representative-position qualification. Exit Reliability capture follows that stage; its method and comparability across ETF/index classes require their own versioned decision. The 1,641 ETF scores remain attached as dated evidence regardless of the new gate's outcome.

The first-pass ceiling is roughly 591 new four-leg probes (2,364 leg symbols) if every unresolved type/term/reference issue is resolved favorably. At PR #33's observed packing limit of ten four-leg probes per bulk request, that is **about 60 option-quote requests**, plus batched reference quotes and bounded retries/fallbacks. Actual requests should be lower after the cheap gates. This is a request-budget estimate, not acquisition authorization or a completeness claim. Capture during a declared market session; do not combine an after-hours stale quote with the 2026-10-01 PR #33 session as if contemporaneous.

## Required report from the experiment

Reconcile all 2,273 classes into mutually exclusive **product/terms excluded**, **identity unresolved**, **dated geometry unavailable**, **reference unavailable**, **market observation incomplete**, **four-leg market observed**, and **acquisition failed** states, with source/date per row. Report index and both ETF-evidence tiers separately. Show class counts and distinct-underlying counts, plus the 1,641 prior ETF score coverage; never label their union “the finished Iron Condors list.” A later production-admission decision will choose which qualified classes Wheelwright may recommend.

**Current limit:** The existing evidence supports 524 dated ETF positives under one fixed probe definition and structural/identity accounting for the rest. It does **not** yet answer how many of all 2,273 classes have a usable market. The market session had ended when this preflight was prepared, so no new quote capture was attempted.
