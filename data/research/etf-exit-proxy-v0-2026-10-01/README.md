# ETF iron-condor Exit Reliability v0 result — 2026-10-01 market date

[Full sorted two-column table](exit-reliability-v0.md) · [CSV](exit-reliability-v0.csv) · [Summary and file hashes](summary.json)

This is a **0–100 quote-surface proxy**, higher meaning more evidence that a fixed four-leg iron-condor-shaped position could be closed near its displayed leg midpoints. It is **not an empirical exit-fill probability** or a trading recommendation. The cohort is the 1,643 Cboe-labeled ETF underlyings in the frozen Pass −1 projection. The result has one scored row for each of 1,641 ETFs; `unevaluable.json` names the two without a usable underlying reference price. Zero means the chosen geometry was absent or the observed quotes did not support the fixed probe, not a failed acquisition.

`capture.py` read the frozen, hash-verified 2026-10-01 exact-root option-symbol listing and captured 2026-10-01 closing-session quotes through Tradier's bulk quote endpoint after market close. The raw option and underlying responses, request times, symbols, HTTP status and SHA-256 hashes are retained in `raw/` and `capture-manifest.jsonl`. All 286 GET attempts returned HTTP 200. `probe-plan.json` records the exact contracts and their quote-request IDs; `probe-details.json` records every per-expiration input/result. `calculate.py` hash-verifies and recomputes the table. `repair_outer_wings.py` records a narrow additional capture after an initial planner bug treated an unavailable target wing as proof that no outer wing existed. The final planner constrains long wings beyond their corresponding short strikes. No request placed or modified an order.

For each ETF, the calculation uses the exact ordinary OCC class/root equal to its underlying symbol, the closest distinct listed expirations to 45 and 75 calendar DTE within 21–100 DTE, and target strikes at 95%/90% of underlying last (put short/long) and 105%/110% (call short/long). The nearest listed strikes are used, with the long wing constrained beyond the short strike. Each expiration's four options were requested **in one bulk quote response**. A missing second listed expiration or unrepresentable four-leg structure contributes zero to that second slot; both slots have equal weight. This is a production-shaped probe, not a Pass 1 risk-comparability rule.

The per-slot 100-point calculation gives 20 points for the fraction of legs with positive, uncrossed two-sided quotes, positive displayed sizes and both side timestamps within 60 minutes of 20:15 UTC; 20 more if all four meet that condition; up to 35 for low summed half-spread concession relative to the narrower wing width (zero credit at 25% or more); 10 for minimum displayed size on the closing sides; 7 for side recency; 4 for minimum leg OI; 2 for mean leg volume; and 2 for target-strike proximity. The two slot values are averaged. Raw null, zero and absent values stay distinct in provider bodies; no missing quote is imputed. The 20:15 UTC comparison anchor accommodates ETFs whose options quote through 4:15 p.m. ET, as described by [Cboe](https://www.cboe.com/notices/content/?id=61722); an ETF that ceased quoting at 4:00 p.m. may lose modest freshness points.

The strongest limitation is that **displayed leg markets are not complex-order exit fills**. The repository's [tastytrade operator record](../../../docs/discovery/tastytrade-iron-condor-operator-learning-2026-09-30.md) documents EWZ and XLE four-leg *opening* fills and working exit orders, but no complete exit fill that can calibrate this table. The observations cover one closing session and two fixed expiration probes, not repeated exit conditions. No ETF was added or removed to make familiar names rank higher.
